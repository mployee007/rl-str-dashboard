## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **Semiconductors dominated.** NVDA, AVGO, AMD, SMCI, and MPWR all make chips — 5 of the top 10. The AI boom turned semiconductor stocks into the decade's undisputed winners.

2. **NVIDIA is in a class by itself.** At ~100x return, it crushed every other large-cap stock. $10,000 invested in NVDA in 2016 became ~$995,000.

3. **Eli Lilly (LLY) was the best non-tech stock.** Weight-loss drugs (Mounjaro/Zepbound) drove a ~11x return — the best among traditional blue chips.

4. **The Magnificent 7 dominates both indices.** NVDA, TSLA, AVGO, NFLX, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, and META collectively drove most of the S&P 500's gains. Without them, the index's returns would be dramatically lower.

5. **Netflix, Apple, and Microsoft all returned 8-9x** — extraordinary for trillion-dollar companies, yet they didn't crack the top 5.

## Methodology

Returns calculated from Yahoo Finance adjusted close prices (accounts for dividends, splits, and spin-offs). Period: September 2016 to September 2026 (earliest available monthly bar within that window), approximately 8.9 years. CAGR = (End/Start)^(1/years) - 1. All returns are total returns in USD.
