## Key Takeaways

1. **Bitcoin is in a league of its own** — delivering a 78% annualized return, over 25x the rate of inflation. However, it came with extreme volatility (multiple drawdowns exceeding 60%).

2. **Tech stocks (Nasdaq 100) crushed broad market** — nearly 18% annualized, driven by the mega-cap tech rally. The S&P 500 itself returned ~15% annualized, handily beating inflation.

3. **Gold surprised many** — with a ~12% annualized return, gold nearly matched small cap stocks. The massive rally from 2023–2025 ($2,063 → ~$3,295) was driven by central bank buying and geopolitical uncertainty.

4. **Traditional diversification still worked** — Emerging markets, international developed stocks, REITs, and even high yield bonds all delivered positive real returns above inflation.

5. **Fixed income struggled** — Investment grade bonds (-13% in 2022 alone) and long Treasuries were crushed by the rate-hiking cycle. Cash/T-bills only recently became competitive as rates rose post-2022.

6. **The "lost decade" for bonds was real** — 10-year Treasuries returned just 0.7% annualized, far below inflation, making this one of the worst decades for bondholders in modern history.

## Methodology

Returns are calculated as total returns (price appreciation + reinvested dividends/interest) on a calendar-year basis from 2016 through 2025. Annualized CAGR is computed geometrically: `CAGR = (End Value / Start Value)^(1/10) - 1`. The inflation benchmark uses the Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), not seasonally adjusted, annual average rates for each calendar year compounded over the full period.
