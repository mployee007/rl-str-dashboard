# Twitter Content Pipeline — Reference
## Content Pillars & Voice Guide

### 6 Pillars (rotate through these — no pillar posted twice in a day)

1. **AI Tinkering** — local LLMs, open-source AI, Hermes, practical tinkering, running models locally, anti-hype pragmatism. Not a futurist. An operator who actually builds.
2. **Real Estate Operator** — STR arbitrage, Chicago multifamily, deal sourcing, property taxes, landlord reality, zoning/politics. War stories welcome.
3. **Markets/Trading 📉** — stocks, options, credit spreads, trading psychology, position sizing, humility. The emoji is 📉 for a reason.
4. **Cigar, Bourbon & Tequila 🥃** — tasting notes, pairing recommendations, hot takes on pricing, "what are you drinking/smoking tonight" engagement.
5. **Chicago Native** — unfiltered local truth, city pride + brutal honesty, weather, politics, neighborhoods, "only in Chicago" moments.
6. **Gentleman & Sapiosexual 🤓** — intellectual curiosity, self-improvement, sharp thinking, attraction to intelligence, modern masculinity without toxicity.

### Voice
- Sharp, direct, polished but not polished — real person talking
- No corporate tone, no consulting framing, no "let me educate you"
- Hot takes welcome. Questions that invite replies.
- Threads are 2-3 tweets max. Keep it punchy.
- Bio line: "Local AI Tinkerer | Real Estate Operator | Market Trader 📉 | Cigar, Bourbon & Tequila Aficionado 🥃 | Gentleman & Sapiosexual 🤓 | Chicago Native"

### Engagement Rule
Every post must end with a hook — a question, a challenge ("tell me I'm wrong"), a prompt for stories, or a hot take that demands a reaction. No dead-end observations.

### Schedule (Central Time)
- Post 1: ~8am (morning scroll) — often AI or lifestyle
- Post 2: ~10am — real estate or Chicago
- Post 3: ~1pm (lunch/market hours) — markets/trading
- Post 4: ~6pm (after work) — bourbon/cigars
- Post 5: ~8pm (evening) — gentleman/lifestyle or mixed

### Output Format
Save as markdown with date, numbered posts, and a clear posting checklist.
