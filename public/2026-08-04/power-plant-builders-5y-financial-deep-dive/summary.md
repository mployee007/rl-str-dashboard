## Key takeaways
- **Best operator + best stock:** **Quanta Services** is the clear winner. Revenue more than doubled from FY21 to FY25, net income more than doubled, and the stock returned **~571%** over five years.
- **Best mid-cap compounder:** **Primoris** posted nearly Quanta-like revenue growth from a smaller base, with net income up strongly and a **~254%** five-year stock return.
- **Turnaround trade:** **Fluor** delivered a **~201%** five-year stock gain despite weak revenue growth and still-negative FY25 net income. The market is pricing a turnaround and backlog quality rather than current earnings strength.
- **Cyclical grower with leverage:** **MasTec** grew revenue materially and the stock nearly tripled, but its balance sheet is heavier than Quanta/Primoris and earnings quality has been more cyclical.
- **Highest-risk laggard:** **Babcock & Wilcox** remains the weakest screen result: shrinking revenue, negative FY25 net income, high leverage, and only **~30%** five-year stock appreciation.

## Bottom-line verdict
If the goal is to find the **best public-market proxy for building out new power capacity**, my ranking is:
1. **Quanta Services** — best scale, growth, and stock execution
2. **Primoris Services** — strongest smaller-cap compounding story
3. **MasTec** — good growth, but more leverage / execution volatility
4. **Fluor** — viable EPC turnaround, but fundamentals lag stock recovery
5. **Babcock & Wilcox** — niche exposure, but weakest financial profile
