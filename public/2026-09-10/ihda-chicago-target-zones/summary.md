## What "Targeted" Means (per IHDA)

> "The federal government has designated certain areas as 'targeted areas' where more home ownership is desired. **Buyers don't need to be a first-time homebuyer. Income and purchase price limits in targeted areas are also higher**, which could open the door for additional buyers to qualify." (ihda.org/lenders-realtors/targeted-areas/)

On the IHDA map: **blue-shaded tracts = TARGETED**, green-shaded tracts = not targeted.

## Caveats / methodology notes

1. **Tract definition:** IHDA uses 2010 census tract GEOIDs (state 17, county 031). The crosswalk to ZIP codes uses the 2010 Census ZCTA-to-tract relationship file, which assigns each tract to every ZCTA it touches. A ZIP is included in the list if it intersects even a small part of any targeted tract.
2. **ZIP coverage varies:** Some targeted tracts (especially large ones on the south/west sides) overlap many ZIPs; some ZIPs are listed for only one targeted tract (small sliver overlap). For practical targeting, focus on ZIPs with **5+ targeted tracts** for high-confidence "mostly targeted" ZIPs.
3. **"Targeted" applies to census tract, not ZIP:** IHDA determines eligibility by tract. A specific address in 60636 might be in a green (non-targeted) tract even though the ZIP is mostly blue. Use the IHDA map tool (or HUD QCT lookup at https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/qct.html) to confirm any specific property.
4. **The 173 suburban Cook County tracts** (out of 370) are not listed in this report; they include parts of Evanston, Oak Park, Cicero, Berwyn, Calumet City, Harvey, Dolton, Markham, Country Club Hills, Hazel Crest, Homewood, Flossmoor, Chicago Heights, Park Forest, Richton Park, etc. (south and west suburbs).
