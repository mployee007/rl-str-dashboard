# 203(k) and HomeStyle feasibility for Chicago target areas

## Bottom line

**For your current stated strategy — buying in Chicago without living in a unit — FHA 203(k) is effectively a no, and HomeStyle is only a maybe for a 1-unit investment property, not for the 2–4 unit house-hack / multi-family play you were discussing earlier.**

The biggest blocker is **occupancy**, not Chicago pricing.

- **FHA 203(k):** primary residence only.
- **HomeStyle Renovation:** can be used for **primary, second, or investment** occupancy, **but the multi-unit flexibility is tied to primary occupancy**. The source we pulled shows **1–4 unit primary**, **1-unit second home**, and **1-unit investment**.
- **Your earlier Chicago targets** from profile context were **60653, 60616, and 60637**, with a **no live-in unit** preference. Under that constraint, these renovation loans are **not a clean fit for multi-family acquisition**.

## What the programs actually do

### FHA 203(k)
HUD describes two versions:

| Program | What it does | Key cap/rule we verified |
|---|---|---|
| Limited 203(k) | Minor / non-structural rehab rolled into the mortgage | Up to **$75,000** in repairs |
| Standard 203(k) | Major rehab / structural work | Rehab must be at least **$5,000** and total value must remain within local FHA limits |

Additional program characteristics from the sourced comparison page:

| Item | FHA 203(k) |
|---|---|
| Min down payment | **3.5%** |
| Min credit score | **580+** |
| Occupancy | **Primary only** |
| Eligible properties | **1–4 unit, condos** |
| Rehab sizing | Up to **110% of after-improved value** |
| DIY work | **Not allowed** |
| Mortgage insurance | FHA MIP; often long-lasting |

### Fannie Mae HomeStyle Renovation
From the sourced HomeStyle guide:

| Item | HomeStyle |
|---|---|
| Min down payment | **3%** for **1-unit primary** homes |
| Min credit score | **620** |
| Occupancy | **Primary, second, investment** |
| Property types | **1–4 unit primary**, **1-unit second homes**, **1-unit investment**, plus condos/co-ops/PUDs |
| Rehab sizing | Up to **75% of as-completed value** |
| Completion deadline | **15 months** |
| DIY work | Allowed, but limited to **10% of as-completed value** and only for **1-unit owner-occupied** homes |
| Reserves | Up to **6 months PITI** if uninhabitable during work |
