## 4. Cook County Land Bank Authority — Commercial Inventory

### Portal Limitations
The CCLBA ePropertyPlus portal (`public-cclba.epropertyplus.com`) is **map-bound** — it only shows properties in the current map viewport. The search bar filters visible results, not the full database. Programmatic API access returned only 1 result because the view was pinned to Dolton.

### What We Found
Only 1 commercial property was visible:
- **14456 Greenwood Rd, Dolton, IL 60419** — Commercial Structure, $20K min bid / $30K ask
- PIN: 29-03-421-042-0000, 18,375 SF lot, 5-22 land use (one-story non-fireproof public garage)
- **Offers were due August 27, 2026 — batch is CLOSED**

### What's in the Full Inventory (per skill reference, Sep 2026)
- **59 Commercial Property** + **6 Industrial Property** = 65 commercial/industrial
- Geographic concentration: Dolton, Riverdale, Cicero, Maywood, Harvey, Matteson, Calumet City, Lansing — NOT Chicago proper
- Chicago proper has only scattered commercial parcels; the CCLBA inventory skews toward south suburbs
- Price range: $350 – $9.99M (mean ~$30K, median ~$7,500 across all property types)

### How to Access Full Inventory
1. **Pre-qualify:** https://cookcountylandbank.org/pre-qualification-application-purchasing-property/
2. **Contact:** (312) 603-8015 | Info@CookCountyLandBank.org
3. **Programs:** Tax Certificate Program (investor path, no owner-occupancy required) and Commercial/Industrial Developer Program
4. **Tax Certificate:** $500 deposit + $1,000 forgivable mortgage (36 months), all back taxes forgiven, up to 9 months to deed
5. **Scavenger Sale:** 1,951 PINs from 2022, of which ~344 are CCLBA-held. Next sale expected 2026-2027

### Bottom Line on CCLBA
The CCLBA is a real pipeline for deep-discount commercial in Cook County, **but the inventory is concentrated in south suburbs (Dolton, Harvey, Riverdale), not Chicago proper.** If you're willing to go to south Cook County suburbs, there are likely 65 commercial/industrial parcels available. Pre-qualification is the first step — the full inventory is only accessible after you're in their system.
