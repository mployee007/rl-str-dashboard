## 9. RISK DISCLOSURES

- **City-owned properties (Milwaukee, Rockford NILBA):** Quit-claim deeds, as-is/where-is, reversion-of-title if rehab not completed on time. Budget conservatively for rehab and carry.
- **Pre-1940 buildings:** Lead paint, asbestos, structural masonry, and environmental (historic industrial uses) are real costs. Always run Phase I ($1,500-3,000). Use EPA Brownfield grants where available (Rockford has one via R1 Planning Council).
- **Gas stations / funeral homes / industrial:** Phase II required. UST removal $100-250K; formaldehyde contamination from funeral homes requires specialized remediation.
- **TIF districts:** Incentives are real (Rockford Midtown, River District North) but application processes take 3-6 months. Factor into timeline.
- **Historic credits:** 20% federal + state (IL, WI, IN have varying programs). Requires preservation architect + National Park Service review — 12-18 month process.
- **Milwaukee DCD constraints:** Wisconsin-licensed broker required, prohibited use list (no liquor stores, pawn shops, etc.), reversion clause, and property must be taxable.
- **Exit liquidity:** Small commercial in secondary Midwest markets is thin. Plan 5-7 year hold. Refinance after stabilization, don't count on a quick flip.
