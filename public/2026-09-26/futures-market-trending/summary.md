## Primary Answer: Crude Oil (WTI)

- **Contract:** CL=F, "Crude Oil Nov 26" (front month), NYMEX

- **Trajectory:** $71.92 → $92.44 over 3 months = **+28.5%**. The 6-month figure is only +2.35%

## Methodology

- Price data: Yahoo Finance v8 chart API (`query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/{SYMBOL}`)
  with adjusted close, intervals of 1d across 1mo/3mo/6mo ranges. Last data point 2026-09-25
  (equity/commodity session close).
- Volume/open interest: Yahoo Finance Commodities page (live table), captured Sept 26, 2026.
- Multi-timeframe consistency used as the "trend" filter (a market must be moving in the same
  direction across 1-, 3-, and 6-month windows, or show a clear accelerating momentum move).
