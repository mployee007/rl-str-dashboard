# Decisions

## 1. Primary market selection: Crude Oil over Sugar

Sugar (+36.8% in 3 months) shows a stronger percentage move than Crude Oil (+28.5%). However,
Crude Oil was selected as the primary answer because:

- **Higher profile:** WTI crude is the most-watched commodity globally. When someone asks
  "what futures market is trending," oil is the headline answer.
- **Stronger confirmation:** Deeper volume (324K vs 63K), open interest closely matching
  volume (healthy ratio), consistent news coverage confirming the narrative.
- **Cleaner structure:** The V-shaped recovery pattern is technically a strong setup (the
  market went down, found support, and reversed sharply). This beats Sugar's more
  grinding/laddering uptrend in terms of "trend readability."
- **Sugar is mentioned:** It's listed as the #2 trending market and the strongest by
  percentage. It's not being ignored.

## 2. Bitcoin as a futures market

BTC=F (CME Bitcoin futures) was included because it's a genuine futures contract on a
regulated exchange (CME) with meaningful institutional participation. Even though Bitcoin
is a cryptocurrency, CME Bitcoin futures are a traditional futures market. Excluding it
would have been an oversight — +42.8% in 3 months with +6.95% in the last month shows
strong, consistent momentum.

## 3. Excluding Natural Gas

NG=F shows an impressive +17.4% in 1 month, but the 3-month figure is -2.75%. This signals
a mean-reverting/choppy market, not a clean trend. A 1-month spike masked by 3-month
flatness is precisely the pattern that gets less-experienced traders into chop. Excluding
it is the honest call.

## 4. Excluding Coffee (down-trending)

Coffee is in a clear downtrend (-25.1% in 1 month) but the question asks for "trending"
markets, not "trending down" specifically. A down-trend qualifies as a trend, but most
users asking about "trending markets" are looking for uptrends. It was mentioned in a
note for completeness but not in the ranked table.

## 5. Why 3 timeframes (1mo/3mo/6mo)

A single-timeframe metric (e.g., 1-month return) risks highlighting a flash-in-the-pan.
Three timeframes provide:
- 1-month: current momentum
- 3-month: medium-term trend validity
- 6-month: structural trend (is this a regime change or noise?)
