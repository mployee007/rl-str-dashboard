## Regulatory Pathway

### License Required
All STRs in Austin **must be licensed** by Austin Development Services Code Compliance. As of **July 1, 2026**, online platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, etc.) are required to verify licenses and remove unlicensed listings.

### Costs

| Item | Amount |
|-----|:------:|
| New STR Operating License | **$836.30** (one-time) |
| Renewal (every 2 years) | **$385.30** |
| Hotel Occupancy Tax (HOT) | Collected by platform; quarterly filing required |

### What's No Longer Required (Oct 2025 Reforms)
- ❌ **Certificate of Occupancy** — eliminated
- ❌ **Proof of insurance** — eliminated
- ✅ **Tenants may operate** with landlord permission

### Timeline
- **Application processing:** 6–8 weeks (single-family)
- **License validity:** 2 years from issuance
- **Recommended:** Submit application immediately upon lease signing

### Key Requirements
1. **Local contact** — must reside in Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, or Caldwell County; available 24/7, 2-hour emergency response
2. **Neighbor notification** — city mails notice to all properties within 100 feet (at renewal too)
3. **Quarterly HOT reports** — platforms collect tax; operator files quarterly report confirming amounts
4. **Information packet** — must be posted in unit (noise limits, parking, trash, local contact info)
