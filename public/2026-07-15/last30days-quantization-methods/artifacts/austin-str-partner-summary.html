📋 Executive Summary

Austin, Texas represents the strongest short-term rental market in the state, driven by year-round events (SXSW, ACL, Formula 1), tech sector growth, and consistently high occupancy rates. Recent City of Austin regulatory changes (October 2025) have made STR licensing significantly simpler and more accessible — including for tenant-operators.

Our analysis identifies viable properties with projected monthly spreads of +$1,000 to +$1,600 after rent costs.

🏛️ Regulatory Pathway

License Required

All STRs in Austin must be licensed by Austin Development Services Code Compliance. As of July 1, 2026, online platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, etc.) are required to verify licenses and remove unlicensed listings.
