**Date of brief:** Nov 2, 2026 · **Status:** Definitive agreement signed, announcement day
**Source of record:** Marvell IR press release #1040 — "Marvell and Lumentum to Combine, Creating a Category Leader in AI Connectivity" (Business Wire, Nov 2, 2026)

Largest tech M&A transaction on record (>AVGO/VMware $69B, MSFT/Activision $69B, Dell/EMC $67B).

## 2. Strategic rationale (company framing)

- **Electrical + optical convergence.** Marvell brings PAM DSPs, DCI/coherent modules, silicon photonics, Ethernet/PCIe switching, custom XPU + XPU-attach, advanced packaging. Lumentum brings high-power/high-speed lasers, **InP compound semiconductor fabs**, transceivers, and **optical circuit switching (OCS)**.
- **"End-to-end extreme co-design."** Copper is hitting physical limits; AI scale-up/scale-in architectures are moving to integrated optics (co-packaged optics, optical scale-up fabrics). Pre-validated, jointly-designed optical subsystems shorten customer time-to-market.
- **Vertical integration of supply.** Owning InP laser manufacturing de-risks the single biggest optical supply bottleneck and supports heterogeneous integration.

## 5. Read-across and second-order effects

- **Optical scarcity repriced upward:** Coherent (COHR) +2% and upgraded by Northland; Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI) upgraded — the read is that remaining independent AI-optics assets are worth more.
- **LITE downgraded by Stifel** on deal terms; Seeking Alpha published both "costly mistake" and "not a mistake" takes — the debate is valuation, not strategy.
- **Peers:** AVGO, ANET, COHR are the natural comps; a combined MRVL/LITE would be the only vertically-integrated electrical+optical merchant supplier at scale.
- **MRVL Q3 FY27 (Dec 1)** is un-affected operationally: Marvell narrowed preliminary Q3 revenue guidance to **±2% around the prior midpoint** (from ±5%). LITE separately reported FQ1'27 results the same morning.

## 6. Risks (bear case)

1. **Price.** ~30x TTM sales, ~45x forward earnings for a business with GAAP losses (TTM net income −$6.6B, largely non-cash/impairment-driven). Paid in stock at a moment when MRVL itself has de-rated.
2. **Dilution.** ~408M new MRVL shares on 877M existing → ~32% dilution; MRVL holders go from owning 100% of a $154B company to 67% of a $225B one.
3. **Fixed exchange ratio, no collar.** LITE holders are long MRVL from signing to close — 18 months of unhedged exposure. This is why LITE fell harder than the exchange value implied.
4. **China SAMR.** The H1 2028 close date (unusually long) telegraphs regulatory drag. Both companies have material China revenue exposure.
5. **Cycle timing.** Optics demand is levered to hyperscaler AI capex; buying at peak-growth multiples with stock is the classic late-cycle error.

**Bull case:** if optical scale-up is the next AI bottleneck, this creates the only merchant supplier that owns lasers → photonics → DSP → switch → custom silicon, with >$1B synergies and year-1 non-GAAP accretion. LITE revenue is doubling; the multiple is being paid on a fast-moving denominator.
