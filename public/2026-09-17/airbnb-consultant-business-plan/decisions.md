# Decisions — Airbnb Consultant Business Plan

## 2026-09-17

### Decision: Business Model Tiers
- **Context:** One-man consultant business; needed a service structure that scales from low-touch to high-touch
- **Options considered:** Flat-fee only, percentage-only, or tiered hybrid
- **Decision:** Three-tier model — Tier 1 (flat-fee listing development, $497-997), Tier 2 (10-12% revenue optimization), Tier 3 (15-20% full-service management)
- **Consequence:** Tier 1 serves as low-risk entry point and funnel into recurring tiers; Tier 3 has the highest $/client but lowest capacity ceiling (8-12 solo)

### Decision: Airbnb-First Customer Acquisition
- **Context:** User specified Airbnb as the primary acquisition channel
- **Options considered:** Paid ads, content marketing, referrals, direct Airbnb outreach
- **Decision:** Direct outreach via Airbnb's messaging platform using a soft 3-message sequence (compliment → value tease → audit delivery) — no hard pitch until value is demonstrated
- **Consequence:** Free acquisition, high-conversion leads, but volume-limited by Airbnb's messaging rules and manual effort required. 100 outreaches → 2-3 clients.

### Decision: Pricing Psychology
- **Context:** Need to anchor value and overcome "what if it doesn't work?" objection
- **Decision:** 90-day performance guarantee (20% revenue increase or Month 3 free); month-to-month contracts with 30-day cancellation
- **Consequence:** Higher close rate, but requires confidence in ability to deliver. Risk is limited to 1 month of unprofitable work per bad-fit client.

### Decision: Solo Capacity Ceiling
- **Context:** One person, finite hours
- **Decision:** Ceiling set at 8-12 Tier 3 clients or 15-20 Tier 2 clients. Revenue ceiling ~$75-90K/year before needing to hire.
- **Consequence:** Clear "when to hire" trigger. Phase 2 milestone: first VA at Month 7-8 to handle guest messaging.
