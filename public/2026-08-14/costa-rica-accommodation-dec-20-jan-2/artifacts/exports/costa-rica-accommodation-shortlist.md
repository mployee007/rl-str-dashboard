# Costa Rica Accommodation Shortlist — Dec 20 to Jan 2

## Trip brief
- **Dates:** December 20 to January 2
- **Travelers:** 2 adults
- **Budget:** $3,500 total
- **Style:** laid back, but with a luxury feel

## Budget reality check
- **Nights:** 13
- **Raw nightly budget:** about **$269/night**
- After typical lodging tax/fees, workable base rate is closer to **~$238/night before tax**.

That makes true holiday-period luxury in **Nosara, Santa Teresa, and Peninsula Papagayo** unlikely within budget.

## Area guidance

### Best vibe fit
- **Puerto Viejo**
- More soulful, romantic, design-forward, and laid-back than resort-heavy Guanacaste.
- Best aligned with the original brief if budget can stretch.

### Best practical fit
- **Papagayo / Guanacaste**
- Easier luxury-adjacent experience and better chance of staying inside budget.
- Less bohemian than Nosara or Santa Teresa.

### Areas that fit the brief but not the budget well for these dates
- **Nosara**
- **Santa Teresa**
- **Luxury Papagayo**

## Recommendation

### If $3,500 is a hard cap
**Best pick:** Occidental Papagayo All Inclusive

Why:
- adults-only format
- most realistic fit on price
- still feels elevated enough versus a standard hotel
- easier to control total trip cost

### If there is room to stretch above budget
**Best style/value target:** Namu Garden Hotel & Spa or a similar Puerto Viejo boutique property

Why:
- closer to the relaxed-luxury feel originally requested
- stronger romance/design character
- better destination personality than a generic resort stay
