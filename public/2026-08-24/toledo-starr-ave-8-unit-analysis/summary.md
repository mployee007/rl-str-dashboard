# 1211 Starr Ave, Toledo, OH 43605 — 8-unit quick investment analysis

## Bottom line
- **Neighborhood fit:** workable for a **cash-flow / workforce-rental** play, **not** a premium appreciation play.
- **Area quality:** older East Toledo block with clear management/repositioning risk.
- **Asking price:** **$290,000**.
- **Likely as-is / lightly stabilized value:** **~$240K–$320K**.
- **Likely ARV after a real turn and rent reset:** **~$325K–$375K**.
- **Stretch upside only if the building is fully renovated and truly supports ~$850/unit rents:** **up to ~$425K–$450K**, but that is not my base case.
- **Verdict:** **NEGOTIATE**, not a clean take at $290K unless financials and unit condition verify better than the listing suggests.

## Why
- Subject is an 8-unit, 6,636 SF building built in 1907.
- Zillow/Zillapi description says: 8 one-bedroom units, 6 rented, average rent $425, exterior needs improvement, one unit needs repairs, newer furnaces, owner pays water/gas.
- Zillow also shows sold history of **$116,000 on 2022-09-16** and a Zestimate around **$175,400**, which is not enough by itself to price a commercial-style 8-unit but is still a caution flag.
- Local East Toledo 8+ unit sold comps cluster well below the ask on a price-per-unit basis.
