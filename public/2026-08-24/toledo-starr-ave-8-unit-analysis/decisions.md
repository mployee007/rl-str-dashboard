# Decision memo

## Recommendation
**NEGOTIATE**

## Price guidance
- **Would not call $290K a clear buy** based on the local comp set.
- **Better target buy range:** roughly **$220K–$255K as-is**.
- Could justify moving higher only if verified trailing-12 and current rent roll support a stabilized value north of ~$325K.

## What would change the decision to TAKE
1. Verified rent roll showing real in-place collections materially above the listing language.
2. Proof that 8 rentable 1BR units are legal and functional.
3. Unit interiors upgraded enough to support market rents closer to $700–$850/unit.
4. Major capex already handled: roof, plumbing, electric, common areas, boilers/furnaces, water lines, sewer, windows.
5. Clean expense history showing manageable water/gas burden.

## What would push this to PASS
1. Deferred maintenance beyond cosmetics.
2. Illegal/non-conforming unit count.
3. Collections closer to the implied $425 average.
4. Heavy vacancy / bad debt / utility leakage.
5. Needed capex that wipes out the spread between ask and realistic stabilized value.
