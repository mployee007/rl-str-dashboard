# CXMT Research — Notes

## Surprising Findings

1. **CXMT's LPDDR6 is already on their product page** — this was not widely reported. It means they're at cutting-edge mobile memory parity.

2. **CXMT ASPs EXCEED Samsung in some Chinese market segments** — completely inverts the "cheap Chinese memory" narrative. The EE Times report of CXMT pricing above Samsung's ~$1,240 for comparable 64GB DDR5 server modules is a data point that deserves wider attention.

3. **ByteDance's $7B, 5-year deal** with CXMT is massive and underreported. This single contract is larger than many memory companies' total annual revenue.

4. **Apple lobbying to undo CXMT restrictions** — this is a significant political signal. If Apple wants CXMT in iPhones/Macs sold in China, US sanctions policy faces real commercial counterpressure.

5. **CXMT evicted SiCarrier (Huawei-linked) engineers from its cleanroom** — CXMT has enough market power to push back against Huawei, its own priority customer. This suggests serious pricing discipline and demand exceeds supply.

6. **CXMT's FY23 gross margin was -113%** — yes, negative more than 100%. The turnaround to +37.8% in 2 years is extraordinary.
