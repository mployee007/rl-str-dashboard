# CXMT Research — Decisions & Analysis Notes

## Key Analytical Judgments

1. **Pricing Paradox:** CXMT is not crashing memory prices NOW. Their ASPs are within 5-10% of incumbents and in some Chinese segments EXCEED Samsung pricing. This contradicts the common "China will flood the market" narrative — at least for this cycle.

2. **The real risk is 2027-2028+:** When the AI supercycle cools, CXMT's state-backed, margin-insensitive capacity becomes a structural price depressant. The question is timing, not direction.

3. **4F² is real but underappreciated:** CXMT's 4F² announcement (Oct 10, 2026) is a genuine technology leap — bypassing EUV restrictions through cell architecture innovation. Samsung doesn't target 4F² mass production until 2028. But mass production ≠ lab demo; yields at scale are unproven.

4. **HBM is the moat for SK Hynix:** SK Hynix's 60%+ HBM market share and technology lead create a buffer. CXMT's HBM yields (~25% overall) and customer base (China-only AI chip makers) are far behind.

5. **Micron is most exposed:** Highest commodity DRAM exposure, no state backing, single-digit FCF yield. Actively lobbying for more sanctions is a tell.

6. **Sanctions have failed to stop CXMT:** DUV + multi-patterning + equipment stockpiling + 4F² workarounds mean export controls have slowed but not stopped CXMT's advance. The DOD military-linked designation in June 2026 is a political signal, not an operational barrier.

## Methodological Notes

- Reuters primary URLs were blocked (iframes). Reuters data was verified through secondary citations from EE Times, SemiAnalysis, and BigGo Finance — all independent sources corroborating the same figures.
- SemiAnalysis is a paid newsletter; some detail may be truncated. The public portions were used.
- TrendForce/Counterpoint market share figures are proprietary but reported consistently across multiple secondary sources.
