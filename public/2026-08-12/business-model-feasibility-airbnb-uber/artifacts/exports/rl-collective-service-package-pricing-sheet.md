# R&L Collective — Service Package & Pricing Sheet

## Executive recommendation
Lead with one core offer: full-service Airbnb co-host / management for houses and townhomes, with optional setup/onboarding services.

## Package 1 — Listing Audit / Strategy Review
**Best for:** owners not ready to commit yet

Includes:
- market/fit review
- listing/performance review if already live
- operational gap review
- management recommendation

**Suggested price:** $150–$350 one-time

## Package 2 — Starter Co-Host
**Best for:** owners who want help with guest communication, pricing, and calendar management only

Includes:
- guest messaging
- booking coordination
- pricing oversight
- calendar management
- review support

**Suggested fee:** 10%–15% of booking revenue

## Package 3 — Full-Service Management
**Best for:** the main offer

Includes:
- listing optimization/setup
- pricing and calendar management
- guest communication
- check-in/check-out workflow
- cleaner coordination
- turnover oversight
- issue escalation
- review management
- monthly owner reporting

**Suggested fee:** 20%–25% of booking revenue

## Package 4 — Premium Full-Service / High-Touch Management
**Best for:** remote owners or more operationally demanding listings

Includes everything in Full-Service plus:
- vendor coordination
- supply/restock oversight
- heavier after-hours support
- more frequent owner reporting/check-ins
- launch support and setup guidance

**Suggested fee:** 25%–30% of booking revenue

## Optional fees
- onboarding/setup fee: $250–$1,000
- photography coordination: pass-through + admin fee
- emergency in-person support: fixed trip fee if applicable
- permit/admin support: fixed fee or hourly add-on
- restocking/admin purchases: pass-through + handling fee if used

## Recommended opening offer for R&L Collective
For first 3–5 clients:
- Full-Service Management: **22%**
- Setup Fee: **waived or reduced** as a closing incentive
- Minimum term: **90 days**

## Pricing guardrails
- Do not price full-service below 20% unless the property is extremely simple.
- Avoid custom scopes for every client; keep 2–3 standard packages.
- Put owner-paid expenses clearly outside the management fee.
- Set approval thresholds for repairs/replacements in writing.

## Ideal fit properties
- houses
- townhouses
- legally compliant STRs
- out-of-state-owner listings
- 2–4 bedroom units with cleaner turnovers that can be standardized

## Poor fit properties
- buildings with unstable STR rules
- owners expecting luxury service at budget pricing
- properties with no compliance path
- owners unwilling to approve smart-entry / cleaner systems / basic ops structure
