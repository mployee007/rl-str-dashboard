# R&L Collective — 90-Day Launch Plan

## Goal
Launch R&L Collective’s Airbnb manager/co-host business with a tight operating offer, first clients, and repeatable systems.

## Positioning
R&L Collective is a boutique STR management partner for owners who want Airbnb income without day-to-day operations, with a preference for houses and townhomes.

## Days 1–30 — Foundation and offer
### Objectives
- finalize offer
- create SOPs
- create sales materials
- build referral channels

### Actions
- finalize packages and fees
- finalize management agreement checklist
- build onboarding form
- create pitch one-pager
- create sample owner report
- create script pack
- recruit cleaners, photographer, handyman backups
- optimize Airbnb/Co-Host profile
- identify 50 target owners/partners

### KPI targets
- 3 referral partners
- 2 cleaner relationships
- 50 outreach attempts
- 5 discovery calls

## Days 31–60 — Pipeline and first closings
### Objectives
- start converting leads
- refine sales pitch
- test onboarding process

### Actions
- run listing audit offer as lead magnet
- follow up with all outreach contacts
- attend local or online investor/host groups
- pitch Realtors and mortgage brokers
- refine objection handling
- onboard first property if fit is right

### KPI targets
- 10 qualified leads
- 5 property audits
- 2 proposals sent
- 1–2 signed clients

## Days 61–90 — Execution and proof
### Objectives
- operate first accounts well
- document proof
- create repeatability

### Actions
- run first turnovers with checklist discipline
- send monthly report like a pro
- track response times and guest issues
- collect testimonial or case study
- refine pricing and scope based on actual workload
- build repeatable onboarding folder and template stack

### KPI targets
- 1–3 active clients
- 4.8+ review average on managed units
- monthly reporting delivered 100% on time
- at least 1 testimonial/case study

## Weekly cadence
### Monday
- pricing and calendar review
- owner updates

### Tuesday
- outreach and follow-up
- vendor check-ins

### Wednesday
- listing optimization / reporting cleanup

### Thursday
- discovery calls / proposals

### Friday
- KPI review
- turnover planning
- issue backlog cleanup

## Success checkpoints
By day 30:
- offer is clear
- scripts are ready
- SOPs exist
- outreach has started

By day 60:
- real pipeline exists
- at least one client close is in motion

By day 90:
- business has proof of execution
- onboarding and reporting are standardized
- R&L Collective can scale deliberately instead of improvising

## Strategic rule
Do not chase volume early. Chase clean operations, clean clients, and proof.
