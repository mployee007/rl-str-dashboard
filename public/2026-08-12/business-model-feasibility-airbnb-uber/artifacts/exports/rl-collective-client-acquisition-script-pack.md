# R&L Collective — Client Acquisition Script Pack

## Positioning
R&L Collective helps owners turn short-term rentals into hands-off income without the day-to-day guest headaches. We handle listing optimization, guest communication, pricing, cleaner coordination, and monthly reporting.

## Core offer in one sentence
You keep the property. We run the Airbnb operation.

## Target clients
- Out-of-state owners
- Second-home owners
- Small STR investors with 1–4 units
- Accidental landlords testing STR
- Owners frustrated with self-management

## Script 5 — Lead qualification call opener
Thanks for taking the time. I’d love to understand three things: the property, what you want from Airbnb, and how hands-on or hands-off you want to be. From there I can tell you whether a co-host or full-service management setup makes sense.

## Qualification questions
1. Is the property already live on Airbnb or still in setup?
2. Is it a house, townhouse, condo, or apartment?
3. Are there any HOA/building restrictions?
4. What city is it in, and is it already licensed/registered for STR if needed?
5. Are you local or out of state?
6. Do you want full management or just help with messaging/pricing?
7. Do you already have cleaners, smart locks, and turnover systems in place?
8. What would success look like in the next 90 days?

## Objection handling
### “I can just manage it myself.”
Absolutely — a lot of owners can. The question is whether you want the day-to-day responsibility for guest messaging, turnover issues, pricing changes, and problem resolution. We step in when owners want the revenue without the constant operational load.

### “Your fee is high.”
That’s fair. Our value is not just sending messages — it’s running the operation well enough to protect reviews, reduce mistakes, improve pricing discipline, and save you time. Bad turnovers and slow responses cost owners more than a solid management fee.

### “I’m not sure if my property is a fit.”
That’s exactly why we start with a fit/audit conversation. We’d rather tell you no than take on a property that doesn’t make sense operationally or legally.

## Close script
If it makes sense, the next step is simple: we do a property and operations audit, review the management structure, and if there’s a fit, we move into onboarding.
