# Notes & Data Caveats

## Data caveats / anomalies
1. **Honeywell (HON) trailing P/E (8.1x) and EPS ($26.10 ttm) are distorted.** A 2026 separation/spin (market cap fell from ~$143B to ~$67B) created a one-time gain. Use HON forward P/E (22.2x) for comparison. Flagged for manual review.
2. **Eaton EPS: GAAP vs. adjusted.** Yahoo "Diluted EPS ttm" = $9.74 (GAAP), but analyst estimates ($13.55 / $16.13) and "Year Ago EPS" ($12.07) are *adjusted* (non-GAAP). GAAP 2025 EPS per 10-K = $10.45. All price-target math uses adjusted EPS.
3. **ABBNY and SBGSY are OTC ADRs** with delayed quotes — prices less current than NYSE listings.
4. **Re-segmentation:** Eaton combined Vehicle + eMobility into "Mobility" effective Q1 2026. FY2025 10-K still reports the five legacy segments; Mobility financials not yet published.
5. **Analyst 1-yr target $479.57** is the mean of ~25 sell-side targets (low $333, high $534). UBS upgrade 9/8/2026 raised PT $450→$515.

## Assumptions used in price projections
- Consensus adjusted EPS: 2026E $13.55, 2027E $16.13 (Yahoo Finance, 22–25 analysts).
- 5-yr expected growth ~10–17% (PEG 2.69 implies ~16.8% on trailing P/E, ~10.4% on forward P/E). Used ~13% near-term decelerating to ~10% in base case.
- Exit forward multiples: base 26x (2028) → 22x (2031) → 18x (2036); bull 28/25/21x; bear 20/16/14x.
- Fair-value anchor for entry prices: ~24x forward = fair; ~20–22x = margin of safety.
