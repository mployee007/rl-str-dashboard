## 3. American Tower (AMT) — Cell Tower REIT with Growing CoreSite Data Center

- NYSE: AMT (S&P 100 + S&P 500); HQ Boston, MA; CEO Steven Vondran (since Feb 2024); 4,866 employees
- **149,686 communications sites** worldwide + **28 US data centers (CoreSite, acquired Dec 2021 for $10.4B)**
- Sold ATC India to Brookfield/InvIT Sept 2024 for $2.5B (exited largest international market)

### Data Center vs Non-Data Center Exposure (KEY BREAKDOWN)

| Segment | TTM Revenue ($M) | % of Total | YoY Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. & Canada towers | $5,179 | 47.3% | Slight decline |
| Latin America towers | $1,776 | 16.2% | Volatile (FX) |
| Africa & APAC towers | $1,547 | 14.1% | Strong (post India divestiture reclassification) |
| Europe towers | $1,012 | 9.2% | Strong organic |
| **Data Centers (CoreSite)** | **$1,133** | **10.4%** | **+11.5% YoY (fastest-growing segment)** |
| Services | $294 | 2.7% | Lumpy |
| **TOTAL** | **$10,942** | 100% | +6.65% YoY |

**Conclusion: ~87% cell tower, ~10% data center, ~3% services.** AMT is **NOT a pure-play data center REIT**. CoreSite has grown 49x from $23M (FY21 stub) to $1,133M TTM, but it remains a minority position. For pure data-center exposure, EQIX or DLR are better fits.
