## 5. Business Description (1-liner)

- **CRWV (CoreWeave)** -- Pure-play AI GPU cloud provider; operates 51 AI data centers in US/Europe; $1.6B Nvidia supercomputer in Plano, TX; largest customer is Microsoft; spun out of Ethereum mining in 2017; Nasdaq-100 component; IPO March 2025.
- **DOCN (DigitalOcean)** -- SMB/developer-focused IaaS cloud (Droplets, Kubernetes, Managed DBs); pivoting to AI inference via Agent Droplets and GPU Droplets (H200/AMD); acquired Cloudways ($350M) and Paperspace; pivoted to "AI-Native Cloud" branding.
- **NET (Cloudflare)** -- Edge/CDN/security/zero-trust network for ~21% of all websites; Workers edge-compute platform; R2 storage, D1 SQL; processes 45M HTTP req/sec; recent 20% layoff to lean into AI productivity; FY25 rev $2.17B.
- **NBIS (Nebius)** -- AI cloud spun out of Yandex N.V. in 2024; full-stack AI infrastructure (GPU clusters, cloud platform, inference tools); $2B NVIDIA investment (March 2026); 300MW NJ data center; also owns Avride (robotaxis) and TripleTen (edtech).

## 6. Key Catalysts & Risks

### CRWV
- **Catalyst:** Near-100% of 2027 capacity reportedly pre-sold; Nvidia backstop; OpenAI deal; massive scale
- **Risk:** $50B+ debt load; bond yields touching 15%; co-founders sold $3.3B in stock since IPO; massive capex burn

### DOCN
- **Catalyst:** 85% managed-services revenue mix; AI inference tailwinds; raised 2026 guidance; $725M equipment financing secured
- **Risk:** Competition from AWS/GCP/Azure; relatively small scale; $1.8B stock offering in March 2026 to fund AI growth

### NET
- **Catalyst:** Industry-standard edge platform; Workers AI growth; AI bot control products; FY25 rev $2.17B
- **Risk:** 20% workforce cut raises execution questions; negative op margin; 49x sales multiple is rich; 2025 lawsuits

### NBIS
- **Catalyst:** Fastest-growing in the group (507% YoY); Microsoft $20B; NVIDIA $2B stake; 300MW data center pipeline
- **Risk:** Highest P/S in the group ex-NET; 822% capex/revenue; -434% FCF margin; thin profit margin
