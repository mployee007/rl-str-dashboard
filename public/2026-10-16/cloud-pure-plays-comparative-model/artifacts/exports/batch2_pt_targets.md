## 1. SPCX — What is it? (CRITICAL IDENTIFICATION)

**SPCX is NOT an ETF and NOT a SPAC.** SPCX is the public common stock of **Space Exploration Technologies Corp., d/b/a SpaceX**, traded on NASDAQ under ticker `SPCX`.

- **IPO date:** June 12, 2026 (recent IPO — ~4 months old).
- **Company:** Aerospace / launch services (Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, Starship in development), Starlink satellite broadband, and an emerging AI infrastructure business.
- **Reporting segments:** Space, Connectivity (Starlink), AI.
- **Insider ownership 45.14%** (Musk + insiders), **institutional 25.85%**, float 2.56B of 13.57B shares (heavy post-IPO dilution of ~42% YoY share count increase).
- **Float is thin (~7% short of float)** — high volatility expected.

This drives an unusual analytical framework:
- TTM EPS is **negative** ($-2.27) due to large amortization, interest expense, and acquisition-related costs from the IPO/SPAC-rumor history.
- We anchor on **FY2027E consensus EPS = $1.99** — the first normalized profitable year.
- Massive capex ($-42.4B TTM) and growing cash (~$100B) reflect build-out of Starlink constellation + Starship + AI data centers. FCF deeply negative today; investment-mode.

**Conclusion:** Treat SPCX as a high-growth, high-capex, hyper-growth operating company. EPS-based valuation understates long-run value because once Starlink cash flow inflects and Starship becomes operational, free cash flow should run materially above GAAP net income. We supplement the EPS-multiple model with a sensitivity note on revenue + multiple compression.

## 3. Methodology

For each company we use the formula:

> **Price Target = EPS_anchor × (1 + EPS_CAGR)^years × Exit_PE**

Three scenarios per horizon:

- **Conservative:** EPS CAGR below consensus, multiple compression below average
- **Base:** EPS CAGR near consensus, modest multiple compression
- **Optimistic:** EPS CAGR above consensus, premium multiple holds longer

**Exit multiples** (rolled from current forward PE / sector norms):
- **2-year exit:** near current trading multiple (anchor stays relevant, growth still visible)
- **5-year exit:** moderate compression (~25–35% off current) as growth matures
- **10-year exit:** converges toward broader-market / sector average (~18–25x for growth tech/industrials; ~18x for mature REITs)

**EQIX special treatment (REIT):** GAAP EPS is distorted by high non-cash D&A on data-center real estate and the 132.7% payout ratio. We anchor on **FFO/share** ($32.32 FY2026E consensus, P/FFO = 30.23x) as the primary REIT valuation metric and cross-check with EPS.

**SPCX special treatment:** TTM EPS is negative; we anchor on FY2027E consensus EPS = $1.99 and use wider exit-multiple compression because the company is in hyper-build mode with FCF deeply negative.

All math is shown explicitly below. All forward estimates are non-GAAP adjusted (stockanalysis.com convention).

## 10. Notes & Caveats

- All forward EPS/FFO figures are **non-GAAP adjusted** (consensus analyst estimates as of October 2026, per stockanalysis.com).
- The "anchor EPS" choice matters: EQIX uses FFO/share (REIT standard); SPCX uses FY2027E EPS (current TTM is negative); GEV/MRVL use the most-recent consensus forward year.
- All exit multiples are judgment-based and benchmarked to current trading multiples plus typical compression curves for the company's growth profile.
- IRR figures are price-only returns. Dividends (EQIX 2.12%, GEV 0.22%, MRVL 0.11%, SPCX 0%) add modest incremental return over 2/5/10 years.
- 10-year horizon assumptions carry wide error bands; treat the 10Y numbers as directional scenarios rather than point estimates.
- Numbers were pulled on October 16, 2026 between ~13:50–15:10 ET; prices moved during data collection but midpoints are used.
- Web search was unavailable during research; all data came from stockanalysis.com (financials, statistics, forecast pages) and Yahoo Finance / MarketBeat news feeds accessed via the agent_search tool.
