## 4. SWOT — Cloud Service Pure Plays

### CRWV (CoreWeave)
**Strengths:** Massive scale (51 DCs), Nvidia super-computer, Microsoft anchor tenancy, near-100% 2027 pre-sold capacity, 4.5x P/S cheapest in peer group, Nasdaq-100
**Weanknesses:** $51.6B debt load, bond yields near 15%, co-founders sold $3.3B since IPO, –180% FCF margin, dependent on Nvidia GPU allocation
**Opprtunities:** AI training/inference secular growth, enterprise AI workloads moving to neocloud, capacity moat means rivals cant replicate quickly
**Threats:** Debt refinancing risk at elevated rates, hyperscalers (AWS/Azure) undercutting on AI compute, GPU commoditization over time

### DOCN (DigitalOcean)
**Strengths:** ONLY profitable cloud pure play (+$235M net, +$16M FCF), 85% managed-services high-retention mix, 45.9x P/E reasonable vs peers, just raised 2026 guidance
**Weanknesses:** $1B revenue is tiny, gross margin lower (57%) vs platform peers, faces AWS/GCP/Azure on every deal, $1.8B stock offering creates dilution overhang
**Opprtunities:** AI inferencing for SMB/developer market, GPU Droplets expanding TAM, managed services upselling existing cohort
**Threats:** Developer spending cuts in downturn, hyperscaler free-tier competition, talent poaching in tight market

### NET (Cloudflare)
**Strengths:** 21% of all websites, Workers edge computing platform is unique, $692M positive FCF, 72.6% gross margin, $125B market cap size advantage, net cash position
**Weanknesses:** 49.3x P/S is 4x pricier than next pure play, negative op marign (–7.6%), 20% layoff (1100 jobs) signals churn or margin desperation, execution risk post-layoff
**Opprtunities:** Workers AI expanding edge inferrence, AI bot control security products, zero-trust enterprise displacing legacy VPN
**Threats:** CDN price wars (Fastly, Akamai), AWS CloudFront/Lambda@Edge direct compete, 49x sales leaves no room for missteps

### NBIS (Nebius)
**Strengths:** Fastest growing (+507% YoY), Microsoft $20B commit, Nvidia $2B stake, 74% gross margin, $5.26B TTM operating cash flow, 300MW N data center under construction
**Weanknesses:** –434% FCF margin is unsustanable, $10.2B debt, thin 3.1% net margin, 822% capex/revenue ratio, require perpetual capital raises
**Opprtunities:** AI cloud compute arms race, Microsoft deal anchoring, Nvidia partnership, European AI sovereign cloud demand
**Threats:** Building an Nvidia-tyed fleet that could depreciate 70%+ if GPU architectures shift, dilution risk, still proving unit economics work

## 5. SWOT — Data Center REITs

### EQIX (Equinix)
**Strengths:** Pure data center (100%), global moat (260+ DCs in 33 countires), high-margin interconnection cross-connects (17.9% of rev), 300+ Fortune 500 tenans, 9yr dividend growth
**Weanknesses:** 26.5x EV/EBITDA is premium-priced, heavy capex cycle (–$2.6B FCF), 162% debt/equity, 133% payout ratio
**Opprtunities:** AI driving interconnection demmand, 52-wek return +19%, expanding into Africa/Malaysia/Thailland
**Threats:** Interest-rate sensitive (REIT structure), hyperscalers building own DCs, new supply coming online

### DLR (Digital Realty)
**Strengths:** 100% data center, fastest top-line growth (+19%), 47.5% EBITDA marign, Strong Buy consensus with +28% analyst upside, Mistral A capacity deal
**Weanknesses:** 84x P/E, frozen dividend since 2021 (4.88/yr), 25.6x EV/EBITDA close to EQIX but with weaker moat, interest coverage only 2.65x, development-heavy with non-stabilized properties at 25.6% of rev
**Opprtunities:** Hyperscaler demand super-cycle, liquid cooling labs, Mistral AI and other AI tenans expanding capacity, multiple expansions (Spain, Paris, London)
**Threats:** Singapore Loyang fire (2024) showing operational risk, new-build DC supply pressure on lease rates, refinancing risk

### AMT (Americann Tower)
**Strengths:** Cheapest of all seven (18.4x EV/EBITDA, 25.5x P/E), highest yield (3.95%), 14yr dividend growth, $3.96B positive FCF, strongest profit marigns (45% op, 31% net)
**Weanknesses:** Only ~10% data center exposure (CoreSite), ~90% cell towers which is lower growth, FX/emeerging-market headwins, 439% debt/equity reflect tower REIT model
**Opprtunities:** CoreSite data center segment growing +13.5% (fastest segment), 5G densification adding tower revenue, data center could become larger mix over time
**Threats:** Tower leasing growth is single-digit organic, emerging-market political/FX risk, if 5G fails to deliver step-change in tower demand

## 8. Final Recommentations

### For Pure AI Cloud Compute Exposure
**First choice: CRWV** — cheapest P/S (4.5x), massive scale, near-100% 2027 pre-sold, Nvidia/MSFT backstop.
**Second choice: NBIS** — highest growth but highest risk. Only for investors who believe the Microsoft/Nvidia deals vindicate the model and FCF turns positive before dilution overwhelms.
**Skip: NET and DOCN** — neither is a pure AI compute play; NET does edge inferrence (small) and DOCN is Just entering GPUs.

### For Quality + Profitability
**Buy: DOCN** — the only cloud pure play with positive net income AND FCF. 45.9x P/E is the price of quality in a sector where almost everything else burns cash. 85% high-retention managed services mix.

### For Platform / Wide-Moat Cloud
**Buy: NET** — if you accept 49x P/S is fair for a platform with 21% web presensence, 72.6% gross margins, and positive FCF. It's the most valuable cloud pure play by market cap ($125B) for a reason.

### For Safe Income + Infrastructure
**First choice: AMT** — cheapest valuation (18.4x EV/EBITDA), highest yield (3.95%), strongest margins, positive FCF. But only ~10% data center.
**Pure data center pick: EQIX** — premium but justified by the interconnection moat. 2.07% yield, 9yr dividend growth.
**Growth pick: DLR** — highest DC REIT growth (+19%) but frozen dividend and weaker balance sheet.

### Bottom Line
> If you can only pick one name from this entire group: **DOCN** for quality + growth + profitability at a fair price. If you want the highest upside (and can tolerate the risk): **CRWV** at 4.5x sales. If you want income with a data center kicker: **EQIX**.
