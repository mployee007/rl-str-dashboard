# Batch 1 Price Target Analysis — MSFT / NVDA / MU / COHR
**As of:** Friday, October 16, 2026 (intraday snapshots)
**Methodology:** EPS CAGR × Exit Multiple (with explicit assumptions per scenario)
**Anchor:** Forward (NTM / FY27E) EPS, with through-cycle adjustment for MU

---

## Methodology Notes

For each company I anchor on the **forward (FY27E) non-GAAP EPS estimate** as the base year (FY26E EPS $19.78 for MSFT / $9.31 for NVDA / $176.55 for MU / $9.42 for COHR), then apply scenario CAGRs and an exit P/E multiple that compresses over time as growth normalizes.

**Key modeling principles applied:**
- **Mega-caps (MSFT, NVDA):** Law of large numbers — CAGR fades substantially from Y2 to Y10
- **MU (cyclical):** Through-cycle mid-cycle EPS basis (~3-yr average of next 5 years), NOT peak FY27E. Current $176.55 FY27E is at cycle peak; modeling from a normalized $50 anchor
- **COHR:** Non-GAAP EPS preferred (acquisition accounting distorts GAAP); FY27E non-GAAP used
- **Exit multiples** compress as: 2Y ≈ current fwd P/E (or slight premium), 5Y ≈ growth-stock norm (20-25x), 10Y ≈ market avg (15-18x) with moat premium
- **Current P/E source:** Trailing PE from stockanalysis.com; **Forward P/E:** based on consensus next-FY EPS
