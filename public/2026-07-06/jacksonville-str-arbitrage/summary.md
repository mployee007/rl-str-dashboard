## Comparable Analysis & Spread Calculation

### Methodology

- **Occupancy:** Default 60–65% based on AirDNA city average (57%) uplifted for premium properties (near beach, pool, historic district)
- **ADR:** Estimated per-property based on location, size, amenities, and local comps
- **Formula:** Monthly STR Rev = ADR × 30 × Occupancy%
- **Spread:** Monthly STR Rev − Monthly Rent
