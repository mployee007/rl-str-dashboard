## 💡 Key Takeaways

### Austin Dominates 🏆
Every top-5 spot goes to Austin. Its strong event calendar (SXSW, ACL, F1) and tech-driven demand support **$200+/night ADRs** with **65%+ occupancy**.

**Top Austin Pick:** The **2BR/2.5BA Pet-Friendly SFH ($2,000/mo)** — lowest rent, highest spread at **+$1,608/mo**. Near SoCo/downtown with pet appeal.

### Dallas is Moderate
Dallas has steady business demand but lower ADRs (~$160). The **Old East Dallas Furnished House ($2,000/mo, +$700 spread)** is the best play.

### Houston is Challenging
Houston's lower ADRs (~$140) make it hard to achieve positive spreads at these rent levels. The best options are near **UH ($2,150 townhome, +$460)** or smaller **1BR units ($2,000, +$418)**.
