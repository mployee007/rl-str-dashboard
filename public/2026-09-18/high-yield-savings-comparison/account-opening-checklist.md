## Option A: CIT Bank Platinum Savings (3.75% APY)

### Step-by-Step

1. **Go to:** https://www.cit.com/cit-bank/bank/savings/platinum-savings-account/
2. **Click "Open Account"**
3. **Choose account type:** Personal or Business (check if they support LLC)
4. **Enter personal/LLC info** (name, address, SSN/EIN, DOB)
5. **Fund the account** — Minimum $100 deposit via ACH transfer
6. **Verify identity** — May require uploading ID + selfie or answering KBA questions
7. **Set up online banking** — Username, password, security questions
8. **Link external accounts** — Add your operating/checking account for transfers
9. **Set up transfer schedule** — Decide if you want recurring transfers
10. **Download mobile app** — CIT Bank app on iOS/Android

### Post-Opening
- [ ] Confirm first deposit cleared (2-3 business days)
- [ ] Bookmark rate page to monitor APY changes
- [ ] Set calendar reminder to check rate quarterly
- [ ] Enable email/text alerts for transactions > $500

## Option B: Ally Savings (3.00% APY)

### Step-by-Step

1. **Go to:** https://www.ally.com/bank/online-savings-account/
2. **Click "Open New Account"**
3. **Choose "Savings Account"**
4. **Enter personal info** (name, SSN, DOB, address, phone)
5. **Fund the account** — $0 minimum; transfer any amount
6. **Verify identity** — Soft pull on credit report (no hard inquiry)
7. **Set up online banking** — Username, password
8. **Create Savings Buckets** — One per property or expense category:
   - Property 1: Vacancy Reserve
   - Property 1: Cap-Ex
   - Property 1: Maintenance
   - Property 2: Vacancy Reserve
   - ... etc.
9. **Enable Boosters** — Round-ups and Surprise Savings for passive accumulation
10. **Link external accounts** — Connect your checking account
11. **Download mobile app** — Ally Mobile on iOS/Android

### Post-Opening
- [ ] Set up 10 recurring transfers (one per bucket category)
- [ ] Enable push notifications for deposits & withdrawals
- [ ] Order Ally Spending Account (checking) if you want same-bank transfers
- [ ] Connect to Zelle for contractor payments
- [ ] Review rate quarterly

## Pro Tips

| Tip | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **Business vs Personal** | If opening under LLC, verify the bank supports business accounts. Many HYSAs are personal-only. CIT may require a business savings product (different rate). |
| **ACH Limits** | Most HYSAs cap ACH transfers at $250K-$500K/day. Not an issue for starting out. |
| **Rate Shopping** | Rates change. Check quarterly. If CIT drops to 3.00%, switch funds to the next leader. |
| **FDIC Limits** | $250K per depositor, per bank, per ownership category. For LLC: $250K total. If you exceed, split across banks. |
| **Tax Reporting** | Interest earned is taxable. Bank issues 1099-INT annually. Give to your CPA. |
