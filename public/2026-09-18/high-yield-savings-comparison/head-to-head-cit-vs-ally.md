## Fit for Real Estate Investors

### CIT Bank wins if:
- You want the **highest available rate**, period
- You're parking lump sums (e.g., rehab funds, reserve escrow)
- You don't need budgeting tools or sub-account management
- You'll transfer funds to a separate operating account for property expenses

### Ally wins if:
- You want to **track reserves by property** (one bucket per property: vacancy, cap-ex, maintenance)
- You value a top-tier mobile app and easy transfers
- You want a checking account connected for daily operations
- You care about the overall banking UX, not just the rate

---

## The Math: Is Ally's UX Worth $375/Year?

On $50,000, Ally costs you **~$375/year** in foregone interest vs CIT.
That's **$31/month** for:
- Savings buckets to organize property reserves
- A-rated mobile app
- Integrated checking with debit card
- Free ATM network
- Round-up savings boosters

**Verdict:** If you have 1-2 properties, CIT's rate wins. If you have 3+ properties and value clean bookkeeping → Ally's UX pays for itself in time saved.
