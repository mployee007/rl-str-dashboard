## Mandate
LYTE seeks capital appreciation through exposure to photonic and optical companies tied to AI infrastructure: optical transceivers, lasers, silicon photonics, optical interconnects, photonic foundries, fiber infrastructure, sensors, and related technologies.

The prospectus says the fund invests at least 80% of net assets in equity securities or financial instruments that provide exposure to photonic and optical companies, and expects significant investment in Asian issuers.

## Structure observations
- The fund is **non-diversified**.
- It **concentrates in information technology**.
- It can use **OTC total return swaps** and forward contracts.
- Current holdings show substantial **Treasury-bill / cash-collateral usage** alongside direct equity exposure and swaps.

## Main risks
- New fund with very limited live performance history
- Highly concentrated portfolio
- Heavy exposure to the information technology sector
- Significant Asia / China-linked exposure
- Swap counterparty, valuation, and liquidity risk
- Potential premium/discount and wider spreads when foreign markets are closed

## Practical read
LYTE is a **high-conviction, concentrated AI-infrastructure subtheme ETF**, not a broad semiconductor or broad AI ETF. It is best understood as a niche bet on optical connectivity bottlenecks in AI data-center buildout.
