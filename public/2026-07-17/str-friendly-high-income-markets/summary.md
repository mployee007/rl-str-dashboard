## STR Regulation Landscape by State

### 🟢 Most STR-Friendly States (No statewide restrictions / state preemption)
| State | Key Law | What It Means |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Alabama** | No statewide STR law | Cities can regulate but Gulf Shores/Orange Beach are STR-friendly |
| **Arizona** | SB 1350 / HB 2672 | State law **preempts** local STR bans — cities can register but not prohibit |
| **Florida** | State law limits local power | Cities can require licensing but cannot ban STRs; beach towns are very open |
| **Georgia** | No statewide ban | Savannah, Atlanta allow with basic registration |
| **Indiana** | No statewide restriction | Indianapolis very friendly |
| **Missouri** | No statewide restriction | KC, St. Louis — very permissive |
| **Ohio** | No statewide restriction | Columbus, Cincinnati permit with registration |
| **Oklahoma** | No statewide restriction | OKC, Tulsa — wide open |
| **South Carolina** | No statewide restriction | Myrtle Beach is STR central |
| **Tennessee** | HB 1256 (limited preemption) | Cities can regulate but not ban STRs outright |
| **Texas** | Tax code requires HOT collection | No statewide STR ban; Houston, San Antonio, El Paso very friendly |

### 🔴 Most Restrictive States (Avoid)
| State | Restrictions |
|-------|-------------|
| **California** | 90-day caps, owner-occupancy rules, strict zoning (LA, SF, SD) |

| **Massachusetts** | Boston: owner-occupancy required |
| **Louisiana** | New Orleans: moratoriums, strict permit caps |

## Detailed City Profiles

### 🥇 GULF SHORES / ORANGE BEACH, AL
- **Regulations:** No STR-specific ordinance. Basic business license required. No minimum stay, no cap, no owner-occupancy requirement.
- **Income Potential:** Peak season (Mar-Sep) nightly rates $250-$600 for 2-3BR. Off-season still solid with snowbirds.
- **Entry Cost:** 2-3BR condos from $180K-$350K. Single-family from $250K-$450K.
- **Why Now:** Booming "third coast" beach market. New developments and infrastructure.
- **Zillapi data:** $305K buys 4bd/3ba 2,180sqft with $3,244/mo long-term rent (STR est: $6,500-$10,000/mo in season)

### 🥇 PANAMA CITY BEACH, FL
- **Regulations:** STR-friendly. Registration + tourist development tax. No ban, no cap.
- **Income Potential:** One of the highest RevPAR (revenue per available room) in the US for STRs. Spring break + summer + snowbirds = 3 peak seasons.
- **Entry Cost:** Condos from $250K-$500K. Homes from $300K+.
- **Zillapi data:** 2bd/3ba 1,102sqft condo at $415K with $3,920/mo long-term rent (STR est: $8,000-$12,000/mo in season)

### 🥇 DESTIN / MIRAMAR BEACH, FL
- **Regulations:** Walton County allows STRs with registration. No owner-occupancy required.
- **Income Potential:** "Emerald Coast" = premium STR rates. Luxury market.
- **Entry Cost:** $180K gets 2bd/2ba 1,051sqft condo.
- **Why Now:** Some of the highest per-night rates in the South.

### 🥈 MYRTLE BEACH, SC
- **Regulations:** Extremely STR-friendly. Traditional vacation rental market. No city restrictions on STRs.
- **Income Potential:** 19M+ visitors/year. Massive tourism infrastructure.
- **Entry Cost:** Very affordable. Homes from $150K-$350K for STR-viable properties.
- **Property Tax:** Among lowest in the nation (~0.5%).

### 🥈 PHOENIX / SCOTTSDALE, AZ
- **Regulations:** State law (SB 1350/1376) prohibits cities from banning STRs. Registration + TPT license required. Scottsdale has some nightly rental rules but Phoenix is wide open.
- **Income Potential:** Spring training (6 weeks), winter snowbirds, year-round events. Extremely high RevPAR in Prime/Peak seasons.
- **Entry Cost:** $138K for a 3bd/1ba 1,238sqft in central Phoenix. Excellent cap rate potential.
- **Climate Risk:** Heat is a factor, but summer pools = summer demand too.

### 🥈 ORLANDO / KISSIMMEE, FL
- **Regulations:** Requires registration + tourist development tax. But no cap, no owner-occupancy. Very STR-friendly for the theme park corridor.
- **Income Potential:** Disney + Universal = infinite demand. Year-round. Best-in-class for STR.
- **Entry Cost:** Foreclosure option at $199.9K for 3/3 near attractions. General range $200K-$400K.
- **Risk:** Market saturation in some condo complexes.

### 🥉 COLORADO SPRINGS, CO
- **Regulations:** Requires STR license ($250/yr). No owner-occupancy requirement (unlike Denver). Cap of one STR per owner in residential zones? — check current. Generally permissive.
- **Income Potential:** Pikes Peak, Garden of the Gods, Air Force Academy. Steady tourism + business travel.
- **Entry Cost:** Homes from $350K-$500K. Higher entry but strong STR performance.

## Methodology
Cities were scored 1-5 on two axes:
- **Regulatory friendliness:** Based on existence of STR-specific restrictions, permit caps, owner-occupancy requirements, minimum stay laws, and state preemption
- **Income potential:** Based on tourist volume, peak season rates, occupancy rates, and overall market demand

The intersection of both 4+ scores produced the final ranked list.
