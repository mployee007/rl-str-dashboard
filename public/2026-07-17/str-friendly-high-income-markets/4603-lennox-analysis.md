| What Changed | Impact on You |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Tenants may now operate STRs** with landlord permission (Oct 2025) | ✅ **You can do this** — get written permission in your lease |
| STRs are now an **accessory use** in ALL residential zoning | ✅ Legal everywhere residential is allowed |
| **Up to 2 STR units per site** permitted | ✅ Both units fit under this limit |
| No more Certificate of Occupancy or insurance proof needed | ✅ Easier licensing |
| License valid for **2 years** (was 1) | ✅ Less paperwork |

| Local contact must be in Austin metro area | ✅ Easy |
| HOT tax: 9% city + 6% state = **15%** (collected from guests) | ✅ Standard, platforms handle collection |

**Bottom line:** Austin is **workable for arbitrage** as of 2025-2026 — but you MUST get the license and have landlord permission in writing.
