## What Makes a City Great for Arbitrage

Unlike buying, arbitrage depends on the **spread** between your monthly rent and your STR income. The math is different:

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|
| **Low monthly rent** | Your cost basis. The lower, the easier to profit. |
| **High STR daily rate** | Your revenue. Needs to be 3-5x the daily equivalent of your rent. |
| **No owner-occupancy laws** | CRITICAL — you won't live there. Many cities require owner present. |
| **No permit caps** | You need to be able to GET a license. Capped cities are dead for arbitrage. |
| **Year-round demand** | Seasonal markets kill your annual spread. Need 60%+ annual occupancy. |
| **Landlord acceptance** | You need a landlord who allows subletting or you negotiate it. |

## Must-Have Lease Clause

For arbitrage, your lease MUST include one of:

1. **Explicit STR subletting permission** — "Tenant may sublet the premises on a short-term basis (less than 30 days) through online platforms."
2. **No prohibition on STR** — The lease doesn't mention subletting or STRs at all (less protected but workable).
3. **Landlord addendum** — A separate agreement allowing STR use, sometimes with a revenue share.

**NEVER** do arbitrage without the landlord's knowledge. You will get evicted, lose your deposit, and may face legal liability.
