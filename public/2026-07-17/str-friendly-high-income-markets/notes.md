## Regulation Research Notes

### States with STR Preemption Laws (Cities CANNOT Ban)
- **Arizona** — SB 1350 (2022) + HB 2672: Most comprehensive. Preempts local bans, limits registration fees.
- **Tennessee** — HB 1256: Limited preemption. Cities can regulate but not prohibit.
- **Florida** — State law allows regulation but limits total bans. Tourist development tax framework.
- **Texas** — Property tax code ensures HOT collection but no statewide ban power.

### Gulf Coast Friendliness
- Alabama Gulf Coast (Gulf Shores/Orange Beach): No STR ordinance. Business license only.
- Florida Panhandle (PCB/Destin): STR-friendly by tradition and economics. Tourism is economy.
- Mississippi Gulf Coast (Biloxi/Gulfport): Generally friendly but smaller market.

### Why Beach Markets Dominate
1. Seasonal pricing power (3x-5x off-season rates)
2. Established tourism infrastructure
3. Local economies dependent on visitors
4. Lower NIMBY resistance (vacation rentals are traditional)
5. Property managers already exist at scale
