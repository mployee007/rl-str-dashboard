# Cook County Land Bank Authority (CCLBA) — Programs Research

**Research date:** September 2, 2026
**Source:** cookcountylandbank.org (live pages)
**Headline finding:** CCLBA does NOT call anything a literal "grant program" by name in its main navigation. The terminology is **Purchase Assistance Program**, funded out of the **Equity Fund**. Press coverage (Austin Weekly News, 10/3/2024) refers to it as "flexible $20,000 grants," so this is the program commonly meant by "the cook county land bank grant."

## 1. PURCHASE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (Equity Fund) — THE "GRANT"

- **Status:** Open (rolling, first-come first-served, while funds last). Last updated 2026-07-28.
- **Description:** Cash assistance for owner-occupant buyers purchasing a CCLBA-listed home. Funded out of the Equity Fund.
- **Funding amount:** **6% of the purchase price, capped at $20,000.** Applied toward downpayment, closing costs, and pre-close costs (appraisal, insurance, inspection). Confirmed by Austin Weekly News (10/3/2024): "grants up to 6% of a home's purchase price, capped at $20,000."
- **Eligible buyer:** Any individual buyer — no income limits, no credit-score minimum imposed by CCLBA (lender rules apply).
- **Required buyer contribution:** Lesser of **$1,000 or 1%** of purchase price (cannot be gifted).
- **Eligible properties:** Must be on the published CCLBA "Eligible Purchase Assistance Properties" list (PDF updated periodically — 2025-08-05 version on site).
- **Occupancy:** Owner-occupied, **primary residence, 3-year minimum**. Buyer signs a Recapture Agreement at closing; if buyer violates (non-occupancy, sale, refinance for cash-out within 3 years) the grant is recaptured (forgiven pro-rata).
- **Layering:** Other grants / down-payment assistance are explicitly allowed.
- **Application package (all required):**
  1. Purchaser Assistance Program Checklist
  2. Completed Initial Application
  3. Notarized Occupancy Affidavit
  4. Buyer-Signed Recapture Agreement
  5. Fully executed contract (buyer + seller)
  6. Lender pre-approval (financed) or proof of funds within last 30–60 days (cash)
  7. Email complete package to **info@cookcountylandbank.org**
- **Processing time:** Up to **3 weeks** to receive award letter.
- **Homebuyer counseling:** Required for Land Bank-exclusive properties (work with participating Homebuyer Counseling Agencies).
- **Property condition:** Sold AS-IS. Buyer may pursue FHA 203K purchase-rehab loan.
- **Key URLs:**
  - Program parameters PDF: https://cookcountylandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Equity-Fund-Home-Buyer-Direct-Grant-Program-3.11.2024.pdf
  - Eligible properties list: https://cookcountylandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/CCLBA-Eligible-Purchase-Assistance-Properties_20250805.pdf
  - Recapture Agreement: https://cookcountylandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Recapture-Agreement-Equity-Fund-6.2.2026-Sample.pdf

## 2. HOMEBUYER DIRECT PROGRAM

- **Status:** Open. Last updated 2026-03-17.
- **Description:** Buyers (or their brokers) apply directly to purchase a specific below-market, fixable home from CCLBA's inventory. Program launched 2017.
- **Eligible buyer:** Prospective owner-occupants. No income limit imposed by CCLBA. Down payment assistance is allowed from outside sources (lender, City of Chicago, Neighborhood Housing Services, IHDA, broker).
- **Eligible properties:** Properties flagged "Homebuyer" on the Interactive Property Viewer (excludes "Certificate" parcels and developer-restricted parcels).
- **Application fee:** Non-refundable **$1,000** (certified check to CCLBA), credited at closing.
- **Earnest money:** Returned if mortgage denied (with lender's denial letter); forfeited if denial is due to misrepresentation.
- **Broker:** Buyer may use a broker; broker earns 2% commission.
- **Multiple offers:** CCLBA weighs occupancy, contract contingencies, down payment, first-time homebuyer status.
- **Property condition:** AS-IS. CCLBA will turn on utilities if lender requests. Inspection report provided (informational only). Purchase-rehab (e.g., 203K) is the recommended loan.
- **How to apply:**
  1. Browse Interactive Property Viewer, filter "Homebuyer Direct"
  2. Click **Apply** → create account → short app → schedule inspection
  3. Click **Offer** on the same listing to submit
  4. CCLBA contacts with status
- **Lending partners:** Listed under Homebuyer Direct tab on site.

- **Status:** **Likely DORMANT** — last news entries are from **2017, 2018, 2019 winners**. No 2020+ activity referenced. Page last updated 2019-10-22. No 2026 application cycle mentioned on the page.
- **Description:** Annual giveaway of one fully rehabbed CCLBA home (3 BR, ~1,000+ sq ft). Past sponsors include Fifth Third Bank. Past winners: Vanessa Johnson, Auburn Gresham / Washington Heights home (2019); Jenaia Hart, Maywood home (2018); Elaine Lee (2017).
- **Eligibility:** Not specified on the page (was driven by application contests).
- **No purchase price or grant amount** — it is a deed giveaway.
- **How to apply:** Through the CCLBA when open; no active link visible.
- **Practical answer to user:** This program appears to have ended after 2019. Reasonable to confirm directly via info@cookcountylandbank.org or (312) 603-8015.

## 4. TAX CERTIFICATE PROGRAM

- **Status:** **Last Scavenger Sale participation: 2022** (CCLBA bid on 1,951 PINS). Scavenger Sales are biennial (Cook County Treasurer). Next participation cycle not announced on the page. Last updated 2026-03-18.
- **Description:** CCLBA purchases delinquent-tax certificates at the Cook County Scavenger Sale and then offers those parcels to buyers with a redevelopment plan. Goal: extinguish back-tax liability for buyers willing to put abandoned parcels back into productive use.
- **Eligible buyer:** Anyone with a viable, sustainable plan that benefits the community. CCLBA explicitly does NOT want occupied parcels — targets abandoned ones.
- **Disqualifier:** If the applicant is the current owner (or associated with the current owner) of the parcel, they are ineligible.
- **Eligible properties:** Parcels in CCLBA's focus communities / target census tracts where CCLBA holds tax certificates. Not yet on the CCLBA-owned inventory — they take 1–3 years from certificate to deed.
- **Cost to buyer:**
  - **$500 non-refundable deposit** for vacant lots (credited at closing).
  - **$1,000 forgivable mortgage** recorded at closing, automatically released after **36 months** if all conditions met (compliance with local building/property maintenance codes, retain title).
  - All outstanding delinquent taxes **forgiven** up to conveyance date.
- **Timeline:** Once agreement is reached, up to **9 months** for CCLBA to acquire deed.
- **Local-government support letters:** Chicago lots may require aldermanic letter of support if applicant lives outside the ward; suburban lots may require municipal letter of support (provided after application, not at submission).
- **Compliance:** Must follow City of Chicago zoning (e.g., Lot Maintenance 7-28-740, Fencing 7-28-750, Grass/Weeds 7-28-120) and all local jurisdiction rules.
- **How to apply:** Via the Interactive Property Viewer / CCLBA sale portal when parcels are advertised.
- **Note:** Total certificates acquired by CCLBA across 2015, 2017, 2019 sales: **26,520**.

## 5. RESIDENTIAL / COMMUNITY DEVELOPER CHANNEL

- **Status:** Open. Last updated 2026-06-08.
- **Description:** Acquisition channel for nonprofit and for-profit developers who plan to rehab or build residential properties. No purchase assistance / grant attached — this is a property-acquisition pathway.
- **Eligible buyer:** Community developers (organizations or individuals).
- **Eligible properties:** Interactive Property Viewer → filter out "Homebuyer" (reserved) and "Certificate" (Tax Cert Program) properties.
- **How to apply:** Interactive Property Viewer → online application per parcel.
- **Pre-qualification:** CCLBA has a Pre-Qualification Form for Potential Property Acquirers on the Resources / Applications page.

---

## 6. COMMERCIAL / INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY CHANNEL

- **Status:** Open. Last updated 2026-06-08.
- **Description:** Acquisition channel for buyers of commercial, industrial, and vacant lots.
- **Eligible buyer:** Business owners or those expanding a business. Pre-Qualification Form available.
- **Eligible properties:** Interactive Property Viewer, "Commercial/Industrial" filter. Parcels marked "Certificate" are reserved for the Tax Certificate Program.
- **How to apply:** Interactive Property Viewer; quarterly **"Working With the Land Bank"** seminar (last hosted 4/30/2025 at Shine Bright Community Center, 8560 S. Cottage Grove Ave, Chatham). Documents needed: Scope of Work (sample), Checklist for making a CCLBA offer, CCLBA Land Transactions Policy.

---

## 7. DONATING / CONVEYING PROPERTY TO CCLBA

Not a buyer program — owners of problem vacant/abandoned property in Cook County can donate it to CCLBA to escape back-tax liability. Listed under Applications & Forms → "Donating/Conveying Property."

## SUMMARY ANSWER FOR THE INVESTOR

**There is no free grant from CCLBA for an investor.** The closest thing CCLBA offers is:

- **Purchase Assistance Program (Equity Fund):** 6% / $20,000 cap, but it requires the buyer to occupy the home as primary residence for 3 years (deed-restricted via Recapture Agreement). **An investor would not qualify.**
- **Tax Certificate Program:** Allows purchase of parcels at favorable terms (taxes forgiven, $500 deposit, $1,000 forgivable mortgage), but the property must be vacant/abandoned, and the buyer must demonstrate a community-benefit redevelopment plan. Multi-year timeline (up to 9 months for deed).
- **Homebuyer Direct, Residential Developer, Commercial/Industrial channels:** Standard property-acquisition pathways with no cash assistance.

For an investor, the realistic pathways are the **Tax Certificate Program** (vacant land, redevelopment plan required) and the **Commercial/Industrial** or **Community Developer** channels (subject to deed restrictions and pricing).
