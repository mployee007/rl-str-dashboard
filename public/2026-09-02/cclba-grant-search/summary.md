## What Is "The Grant"?

The **Purchase Assistance Program** (funded by the CCLBA Equity Fund). It's the only CCLBA program that delivers cash to buyers. The media and public call it a "grant" but CCLBA terms it "purchase assistance."

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Amount** | 6% of purchase price, **capped at $20,000** |
| **Use** | Downpayment, closing costs, pre-close costs (appraisal, inspection, insurance) |
| **Eligibility** | ANY buyer — no income limits, no credit score minimum from CCLBA |
| **Buyer contribution** | Lesser of $1,000 or 1% of purchase price (cannot be gifted) |
| **Occupancy requirement** | **Owner-occupied primary residence for 3 years** (Recapture Agreement) |
| **Status** | OPEN — rolling, first-come first-served while funds last |
| **Processing** | Up to 3 weeks for award letter |
| **Apply** | Email complete package to info@cookcountylandbank.org |
| **Program PDF** | https://cookcountylandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Equity-Fund-Home-Buyer-Direct-Grant-Program-3.11.2024.pdf |
| **Eligible Properties PDF** | https://cookcountylandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/CCLBA-Eligible-Purchase-Assistance-Properties_20250805.pdf |

## ⚠️ Critical Catch for Investors

The 3-year owner-occupancy Recapture Agreement makes this program **unavailable to investors who won't live in the property**. If you sell, refinance for cash-out, or fail to occupy within 3 years, the full grant amount is recaptured.

## Investor-Friendly Alternative: Tax Certificate Program

Since the Purchase Assistance grant requires owner-occupancy, the better path for R&L Collective is the **Tax Certificate Program**:

- **What:** CCLBA acquires delinquent tax certificates at the Cook County Scavenger Sale and offers parcels to buyers with redevelopment plans
- **Cost:** $500 deposit + $1,000 forgivable mortgage (released after 36 months compliance)
- **Tax benefit:** All outstanding delinquent taxes forgiven
- **Eligibility:** Vacant/abandoned parcels only; cannot be current owner
- **Timeline:** Up to 9 months for CCLBA to acquire deed
- **Status:** Last participation 2022 (biennial); next cycle not yet announced
- **Total CCLBA certificates acquired:** 26,520 across 2015/2017/2019 sales

Also available: **Residential/Community Developer channel** (no grant, but access to developer-restricted parcels).
