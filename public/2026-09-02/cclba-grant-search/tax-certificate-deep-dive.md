# CCLBA Tax Certificate Program — Deep Dive

**Date:** September 2, 2026  
**Source:** cookcountylandbank.org/taxcertificateprogram (live), CCLBA scavenger sale data, Cook County Treasurer site

---

## Executive Summary

The Tax Certificate Program is CCLBA's mechanism for acquiring vacant/abandoned tax-delinquent parcels through the Cook County Scavenger Sale and then transferring them to buyers with redevelopment plans. Unlike the Purchase Assistance grant, this program has **no owner-occupancy requirement**, making it the primary CCLBA pathway for investors.

**Bottom line for R&L Collective:** This is your best CCLBA entry point. You get parcels with back taxes forgiven, $500 deposit, $1,000 forgivable mortgage — but the timeline is slow (up to 9 months to deed from the 2022 sale) and the next scavenger sale cycle isn't confirmed yet.

---

## 1. How the Scavenger Sale Works

### The Tax Delinquency Pipeline

```
Annual Tax Sale → Unpurchased → After 3+ years → Scavenger Sale (biennial)
```

1. **Annual Tax Sale:** Each year, Cook County offers unpaid property taxes as certificates. Private buyers can purchase them.
2. **Unsold certificates:** Properties nobody wants (bad location, excessive back taxes, poor condition) go unsold.
3. **Scavenger Sale:** After 3+ years of delinquency, these "forfeited" parcels go to the **biennial Scavenger Sale** conducted by the Cook County Treasurer.
4. **CCLBA bids:** The Land Bank bids on parcels in its focus communities. It does NOT initially own them — it holds the tax certificate.

### What a Tax Certificate Actually Is

> "This certificate does not give ownership to the tax buyer, but it provides the option for the tax buyer to obtain ownership of the property if the current owner has abandoned the property or does not wish to pay the remaining taxes."

CCLBA holds the certificate, then takes the property to deed (1-3 years), then offers it for public sale.

## 3. Buyer-Side: How to Acquire a Tax Certificate Parcel

### Step-by-Step Process

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Find parcels on the Interactive Property Portal (filter for "Certificate" layer) | Now |
| 2 | Identify parcels of interest with a redevelopment plan | — |
| 3 | Submit application via CCLBA sale portal | — |
| 4 | **Aldermanic/municipal support letter** (if applicable — see below) | After application |
| 5 | CCLBA reviews plan for "viable and sustainable plan that benefits the community" | — |
| 6 | Sign terms sheet if approved | — |
| 7 | Pay **$500 non-refundable deposit** (credited toward closing) | At terms sheet |
| 8 | CCLBA acquires deed from tax certificate | **Up to 9 months** |
| 9 | Closing: $1,000 forgivable mortgage recorded; all back taxes extinguished | At closing |
| 10 | Own and maintain property for 36 months. Mortgage auto-releases if compliant. | 3 years post-close |

### Costs to Buyer

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Deposit | **$500** | Non-refundable but credited at closing |
| Forgivable Mortgage | **$1,000** | Recorded at closing; auto-released after 36 months if all conditions met |
| Back Taxes | **$0** | All outstanding delinquent taxes forgiven up to conveyance date |
| Ongoing Property Taxes | Annual | New owner responsible going forward |
| **Total upfront** | **$500** | That's it |

### Eligibility

- **Anyone** with a "viable and sustainable plan that benefits the community as a whole"
- **CANNOT** be the current owner or associated with the current owner
- **MUST target abandoned/unoccupied parcels** — CCLBA explicitly states: "The Land Bank is NOT interested in acquiring parcels that are occupied"
- No income limits, no credit score minimum, no owner-occupancy requirement

## 4. Special Requirements

### Aldermanic/Municipal Support Letters

| Scenario | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| **Chicago lot + buyer outside the ward** | Letter of support from local alderman |
| **Suburban lot + buyer outside municipality** | Letter of support from municipality |
| **Same-ward/same-municipality buyer** | Not explicitly required |

These letters are **not required at application time** — CCLBA provides further instructions after application.

### Local Compliance

Within City of Chicago, must abide by:

| Code | Subject |
|------|---------|
| [7-28-740](https://chicagocode.org/7-28-740/) | Lot Maintenance |
| [7-28-750](https://chicagocode.org/7-28-750/) | Fencing Requirements |
| [7-28-120](https://chicagocode.org/7-28-120/) | Grass/Weeds Maintenance |
| [17-2-0200](https://chicagocode.org/17-2-0200/) | Zoning Ordinance (permissible uses) |

Similar rules apply in suburban jurisdictions.

### Forgivable Mortgage Terms

- **Amount:** $1,000
- **Term:** 36 months
- **Conditions:** Maintain property in compliance with all local building and property maintenance codes AND hold title throughout the term
- **Release:** Automatic after 36 months if all conditions satisfied
- **Purpose:** CCLBA's enforcement mechanism to ensure the buyer doesn't abandon the parcel

## 5. Deed Restrictions & Resale

The CCLBA page does NOT explicitly describe deed restrictions on resale for tax certificate parcels (unlike the Purchase Assistance Program's 3-year occupancy Recapture Agreement). However:

- The 36-month forgivable mortgage effectively functions as a holding requirement
- CCLBA's "Court Actions to Enforce Deed Restriction" page (under Applications & Forms) suggests some parcels carry deed restrictions
- Best practice: Request the specific terms sheet for any parcel before committing

## 7. Comparison: Tax Certificate vs. Purchase Assistance Grant

| Feature | Tax Certificate Program | Purchase Assistance Grant |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Buyer type** | Investors OK | Owner-occupant only |
| **Occupancy requirement** | None | 3-year primary residence |
| **Property condition** | Vacant/abandoned | Residential structures |
| **Cash assistance** | None (taxes forgiven) | 6% up to $20,000 |
| **Upfront cost** | $500 deposit | Lesser of $1,000 or 1% |
| **Deed restrictions** | 36-month forgivable mortgage | 3-year Recapture Agreement |
| **Timeline to deed** | Up to 9 months | Immediate (buy from inventory) |
| **Back taxes** | Forgiven | N/A (CCLBA already owns) |
| **Redevelopment plan required** | Yes | No |
| **Availability** | Biennial, pipeline-dependent | Rolling, inventory-dependent |
