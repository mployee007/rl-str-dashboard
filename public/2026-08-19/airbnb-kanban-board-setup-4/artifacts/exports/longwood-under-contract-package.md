## Recommended play

### My recommendation
Try to get this tied up at **$58,000 initial offer**, with a realistic contract target of **$60,000–$62,000**.

### Why
- The deal still works at the current ask **only if rehab stays moderate**.
- Because the property is **tenant occupied** and marketed **as-is / needs TLC**, you need margin for hidden repairs and delayed access.
- The cleanest way to win this without overpaying is to pair a **serious but sub-ask offer** with **fast timing** and **clean contingencies**.

### Price guidance
- **Open:** **$58,000**
- **Likely get-done range:** **$60,000–$62,000**
- **Soft ceiling before contractor walk:** **$62,000**
- **Only pay $65,000** if walkthrough confirms rehab is roughly **$35K or less** and there are no major systems / moisture / foundation surprises.

## Offer terms to use

### Core economics
- **Purchase price:** **$58,000** initial
- **Earnest money:** **$1,500** within 1 business day of acceptance
- **Closing:** **21–30 days** from acceptance
- **Property condition:** **As-is**, subject to inspections and contractor due diligence

### Contingencies you want in writing
1. **Inspection contingency** — at least **10 calendar days** after acceptance
2. **Contractor access contingency** — buyer may bring licensed contractors / inspectors during due diligence
3. **Document contingency** — seller to provide within **3 business days**:
   - current lease
   - tenant payment ledger
   - security-deposit amount held
   - utility responsibility breakdown
   - any known code notices
4. **Appraisal / financing contingency** if you are not closing with true cash
5. **Clear title / standard title commitment**
6. **No new leases / no material change** before closing without buyer approval

### Extra tenant-occupied protection
Add language requiring:
- tenant lease and ledger review during inspection period
- same tenant and same rent terms through closing unless buyer agrees otherwise
- seller to disclose any delinquency, eviction history, or habitability issue known to seller
