## 3) On-site contractor walkthrough punch list
Use this in order. The goal is to decide whether this is a **$30K house**, a **$40K house**, or a **$55K+ trap**.

### A. Exterior / structure
- Roof age, layers, soft decking, visible sagging
- Gutters/downspouts draining away from house
- Foundation cracks, step cracks, bowing, settlement, water entry signs
- Siding rot / broken panels / exposed sheathing
- Porch / stairs / railings / trip hazards
- Grading around perimeter
- Chimney condition if present

**Red flags:** active settlement, major roof replacement, porch rebuild, water intrusion at foundation.

### B. Basement / moisture
- Standing water or active seepage
- Efflorescence, mold smell, damp walls
- Sump pump present / working
- Floor drain backups
- Condition of joists, sill plate, beams

**Red flags:** chronic water, rotted framing, major mold remediation, failing supports.

### C. Mechanical systems
- Furnace age and function
- AC condenser present / working
- Water heater age and venting
- Visible plumbing leaks
- Galvanized / cast iron / mixed plumbing issues
- Sewer backup signs or recommendation to scope line

**Red flags:** dead furnace + dead AC + old water heater + sewer uncertainty all at once.

### D. Electrical
- Panel size / brand / condition
- Open junction boxes
- Ungrounded outlets / missing GFCIs
- Knob-and-tube or unsafe legacy wiring
- Exposed splices or amateur work

**Red flags:** obsolete panel, widespread rewiring need, service upgrade.

### E. Windows / doors
- Number of failed windows
- Broken seals / rot / poor operation
- Exterior doors secure and weather-tight

### F. Kitchen
- Cabinet salvageable or full replace?
- Countertops usable?
- Sink plumbing condition
- Appliance package needed?
- Floor softness around sink

### G. Bathroom
- Tub/shower condition
- Subfloor softness around toilet/tub
- Vanity, toilet, vent fan, tile condition
- Signs of active leaks behind walls / below bath

### H. Interior finishes
- Full paint needed?
- Flooring replacement percentage
- Drywall cracks / patching / ceiling damage
- Trim / doors / hardware completeness
- Smoke / pet / trash damage

### I. Occupancy / turnover risk
- Since property is **tenant occupied**, verify:
  - current lease amount
  - payment history
  - deposit held
  - move-out timing
  - any code complaints or habitability issues
  - whether interior condition in photos matches actual unit condition

**Big risk:** tenant-occupied distressed properties often hide deferred maintenance the photos do not show.

## 4) Fast contractor scoring rule
After walkthrough, sort the house into one bucket:

### Bucket 1 — **TAKE**
- Roof has life left
- No foundation movement
- Mechanicals mostly functional
- No sewer surprise
- Kitchen/bath are refresh, not full rebuild
- Rehab comes in around **$25K–$35K**

**Action:** move fast at or near current ask.

### Bucket 2 — **NEGOTIATE**
- One major system hit plus cosmetics
- Moderate water/moisture remediation
- Partial roof / HVAC / electrical work needed
- Rehab around **$35K–$45K**

**Action:** negotiate to protect margin, ideally under **$60K–$62K**.

### Bucket 3 — **PASS**
- Multiple major systems dead
- Foundation / sewer / heavy moisture issues
- Full gut kitchen + bath + electrical + roof stack-up
- Rehab **$50K+**

**Action:** pass unless purchase price gets cut hard.
