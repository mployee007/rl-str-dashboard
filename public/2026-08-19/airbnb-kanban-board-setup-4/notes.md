## User Context
- User asked for deep research on the best real estate markets in 2026 for 1-4 unit homes.
- User then asked for a deep analysis of the Rockford, IL market.
- User prefers structured, data-dense, verdict-driven output.

## Key Decisions
- Framed the national question around small residential investors rather than only owner-occupants.
- Weighted markets on affordability support, rent demand, job/income growth, supply discipline, and liquidity.
- Used Search MCP outputs first, then extracted readable content from the cited pages.
- Treated Rockford as a yield-and-selectivity question, not an appreciation-first story.

## Observations
- 2026 is a poor environment for appreciation-only underwriting.
- The best opportunities split into two groups: large liquid metros and smaller high-cap-rate Midwest/Mid-Atlantic markets.
- Chicago remains unusually relevant because its housing stock suits true 2-4 unit strategies better than many headline-growth metros.
- Rockford stands out because the rent-growth / vacancy / cap-rate mix is stronger than its reputation suggests.
- Rockford's city-level fundamentals are investable, but the market is too uneven for blind citywide buying.

## Rockford-specific evidence captured
- Buildium ranked Rockford #4 among up-and-coming real estate markets for 2026.
- Buildium cited 4.4% asking-rent growth, 4.0% multifamily vacancy, 8.1% cap rate, and $172,853 average home value.
- Census QuickFacts showed 2025 population of 147,384, median gross rent of $985, median owner value of $129,000, median household income of $54,752, and poverty rate of 21.3%.
- Rockford official site noted release of a 2026 Infill Planning & Development Guide aimed at developers, builders, investors, and property owners.
