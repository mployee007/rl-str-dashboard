# Notes

## Model assumptions (for reference / Re-tuning)
- **Home (8 kids):** tuition $1,450–$1,700/mo; opex = food/supplies $55/child + insurance $90 + utilities $150 + misc. "Net" = owner's own income (implicit labor).
- **Group home (12 kids):** tuition $1,400–$1,700/mo; 1 assistant @ $17/hr × ~120 hrs + 12% payroll tax.
- **Center (40 kids):** 8 infant + 10 toddler + 10 twos + 12 preschool; 8 teachers + 1 director + 1 floater; blended $19/hr teachers, $27/hr director; payroll ×1.18; rent $20–24/sf/yr on 5,200 sf; food $110/child; supplies $45/child; insurance $1.6K/mo; registration fee $125/child.
- Tuition anchors: Willow Tree (Chicago) infant $1,907 / toddler $1,676 / twos $1,791; LadyBug IL preschool avg $1,437.

## To verify locally before committing
- Exact City of Chicago BACP license fees + food-service certification (City + Cook County).
- Zoning for home/group-home in the target address.
- Real rent $/sf in target neighborhood (I used $22/sf, mid-range).
- Live CCAP rate sheet (IDHS) — rates update; the ~$67/day figure is from a 2026 secondary source.
- Whether a seller's/landlord's existing DCFS license is transferable (big capital save on a center).
