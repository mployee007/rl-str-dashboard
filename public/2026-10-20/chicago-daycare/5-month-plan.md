# 5-Month Launch Plan — Group Day Care Home (12 kids) · Chicago, IL

**Target:** Licensed Group Day Care Home · Capacity 12 children · Owner + 1 assistant
**Startup budget:** ~$18,000 · **Owner income target (moderate scenario):** ~$121K/yr
**Critical-path risk:** DCFS background checks (2–6 wk) + home visit scheduling (2–8 wk) can kill the timeline. Front-load ALL government interactions.

---

## MONTH 1 — Foundation & Paperwork (Week 1–4)

**Theme:** Launch every government process in parallel. Nothing else matters until the DCFS clock starts ticking.

| # | Task | Owner | Cost | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Form LLC** (Illinois Secretary of State — online, ~$150 filing fee) | You | $150 | None |
| 2 | **Get EIN** from IRS (free, online, instant) | You | $0 | #1 |
| 3 | **Open business bank account** — keep all daycare $ separate | You | $0 | #1, #2 |
| 4 | **Complete DCFS ONLINE LICENSING ORIENTATION** (sunshine.dcfs.illinois.gov — free, a few hours) | You | $0 | None — DO THIS WEEK 1 |
| 5 | **Initiate background checks** for ALL household members 13+ (DCFS portal — fingerprinting + CANTS). This is the single biggest timeline variable. DO THIS WEEK 1. | You + household | ~$50–75/person | None |
| 6 | **Verify zoning** — call 311 or check City of Chicago zoning map for home-based business / childcare use at your address. If zoning is a problem, you need to know NOW. | You | $0 | None |
| 7 | **Order radon test kit** — short-term (48+ hrs; IL DCFS requires radon testing as part of the home visit). Buy a 2-pack, run one now, keep one for retest. | You | ~$30 | None |
| 8 | **Begin insurance quotes** — call 3+ carriers that specialize in home child care (e.g., Markel, Hiscox, Philadelphia, local agent). Daycare liability is specialized — don't use a general homeowner's rider. | You | $0 | #1 |
| 9 | **Define your program** — ages served, daily hours, philosophy (play-based / Montessori / bilingual / etc.). This drives everything else (pricing, space layout, marketing copy, handbook). | You | $0 | None |
| 10 | **Begin space assessment** — walk every room with the DCFS standards. List: what needs to change to pass inspection? (egress, hand-washing sink access, separate sick-child area, outdoor space, fence, toilet training set-up, food-prep surface). | You | $0 | None |

**Month 1 cost:** ~$400–$600 (LLC, background checks, radon kit, misc).
**Month 1 deliverable:** LLC + EIN + bank account. DCFS orientation completed. Background checks submitted. Zoning verified. Program scope defined. Space punch list written.

**Theme:** The house becomes a daycare. Also: find your assistant, because group-home licensing requires backup adult coverage.

| # | Task | Cost | Dependency |

| 15 | **Post job listing for assistant** — Indeed, local ECE Facebook groups, community college ECE programs, church bulletins. Write a clear job description: hours, pay, qualifications (HS diploma min; 15 hrs in-service/yr; must pass background check). | $0 | #9 |
| 16 | **Begin assistant interviews** — do background check on finalists BEFORE hiring. | $0 | #15 |
| 17 | **Apply for City of Chicago business license** (BACP — Small Business Center can advise). | ~$250 | #1 |

## Critical Path — What CAN Delay You

| Item | Normal timeline | If it goes wrong | Your buffer |
|---|---|---|---|
| DCFS background checks | 2–4 weeks | 6–8 weeks (fingerprint reject, backlogs) | Submit DAY 1 |
| DCFS home visit scheduling | 2–4 weeks after app | 6–8 weeks in busy districts | Submit app as soon as background checks clear — don't wait for anything else |
| Radon mitigation | N/A | 2–4 weeks + $800–$2,500 if levels are high | Test Month 1 |
| Zoning issue | 1 week to check | If denied: appeal 2–6 months, or find new address | Verify WEEK 1 |
| Assistant hiring | 2–4 weeks | Can take 6+ weeks in Chicago's tight labor market | Start interviewing Month 2 |
| Insurance binding | 1–2 weeks | Specialty insurers sometimes require 2–3 weeks for underwriting | Start quotes Month 1 |
| City of Chicago BACP license | 2–4 weeks | Can stretch to 6–8 weeks | Apply Month 2 |

## Post-Launch (Month 6–12) — What To Watch

1. **Enrollment ramp rate** — if you're not at 6+ enrolled by Month 6, aggressive marketing needed.
2. **Assistant retention** — ECE turnover is high. Pay competitively ($17+/hr) and treat them as a partner, not an employee.
3. **CCAP billing** — get the rhythm; late claims = lost revenue.
4. **DCFS renewal window** — 6 months before license expires, forms arrive. Submit 3 months before. Don't let this lapse.
5. **Group-home → center decision** — if your waitlist hits 20+ consistently for 3 months, THEN start scouting center space. Not before.
