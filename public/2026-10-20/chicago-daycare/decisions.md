# Decisions & Verdicts

## 1. Which business model to enter?
**Decision: Day Care Home → Group Home → Center (in that order).**
- The center tier only breaks even at 85%+ enrollment and carries $484K upfront + a 9-year payback. Not a first-move.
- The group home is the highest-leverage model: paid staff (real business), 60–80% margins, owner income $121K–$185K/yr, <6-month payback.

## 2. Startup budget
**Decision: approve ~$7K (home) now; stage ~$18K (group home) when waitlisted.**
- Center budget ($484K) is a later, separately-funded decision — only on waitlist depth of 30+.

## 3. Pricing
**Decision: price at/above neighborhood comps (infant ~$1,900/mo), not below.**
- Chicago scarcity is real (20% of families spend >$30K/yr on care). Underpricing only costs margin and signals low quality.
- Offer CCAP acceptance to fill otherwise-empty seats at ~$1,450/mo equivalent.

## 4. Marketing
**Decision: hyper-local, referral-first. Treat the waitlist as the core asset.**
- Win a ZIP code; build the waitlist before you open.

- Whether to operate personally or hire a director to run it (affects which tier makes sense).
