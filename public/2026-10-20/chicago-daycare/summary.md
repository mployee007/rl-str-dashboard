# Summary — Starting a Daycare in Chicago

**Bottom line:** Childcare in Chicago is profitable, but the profit is in the **home/group-home tiers**, not big centers. Start small, graduate only on waitlist depth.

## The three models at a glance (all figures modeled, Chicago)

| Tier | Capacity | Startup | Net/yr (moderate) | Payback | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day Care Home | 8 kids | ~$7K | **$98,700** (owner income) | <2 mo | ✅ Start here |
| Group Day Care Home | 12 kids | ~$18K | **$121,382** + paid staff | <6 mo | ✅ Best leverage |
| Day Care Center | 40 kids | ~$484K | **—$87,290** (loses money at 75% fill) | 9.3 yr at 95% fill | ⚠️ Only on waitlist depth |

**Headline finding:** a 40-child Chicago center **loses $7K–$19K/month below ~80% enrollment.** Labor (DCFS ratios) is ~55% of cost. This is why most new centers fail — a long lease + full staffing before enrollment ramps.

## Key facts (sourced)
- **License:** DCFS (moving to IL Dept. of Early Childhood). Required to care for **more than 3 unrelated children under 12. Free, valid 3 years.**
- **Ratios:** infant 1:4 · toddler 1:5 · preschool 1:10.
- **Chicago tuition:** infant ~$1,907/mo (Willow Tree, actual) · IL avg infant $1,650/mo, preschool $1,437/mo.
- **CCAP (subsidized):** up to ~$67/day for an infant in Cook Co. — free demand, fills empty seats.
- **Startup:** home $7K · group home ~$18K · center ~$484K (build $150–$450/SF).

## Recommendation
1. **Day Care Home first** (buy a ~$64K–$141K/yr job for $7K).
2. **Graduate to Group Home** when you have a waitlist (owner income $121K–$185K/yr, + paid assistant).
3. **Center later** — only once you have 30+ on a waitlist and a trusted director.

## Marketing (60-sec version)
Win a **neighborhood/ZIP**, not "Chicago." → Google Business Profile + waitlist landing page → tour funnel (30–40% tour→enroll) → referral credits → pediatrician/OBGYN + employer partnerships → differentiate on bilingual / extended-hours / Montessori. Budget $60–$80/mo (home) to $300–$500/mo (center, after launch).
