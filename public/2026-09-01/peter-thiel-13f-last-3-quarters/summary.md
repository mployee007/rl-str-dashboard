## Observations

1. **Complete Liquidation in Q4 2025:** Thiel sold his entire ~$212M public portfolio (NVDA, TSLA, Vistra) in late 2025. Both NVDA and TSLA were near/at highs at the time.

2. **Two-Quarter Cash Hiatus:** Thiel Macro sat in cash for a full 6 months (Q4 2025 and Q1 2026), entirely out of public equities.

3. **Massive Q2 2026 Rebuild — Utilities & Energy Bet:** The Q2 2026 portfolio is ~2x larger than the previous peak (~$212M in Q3 2025). The allocation is overwhelmingly defensive/energy-focused:
   - **~71% in utilities + independent power producers** (AEP, DTE, FE, CMS, Vistra Corp, Vista Energy)
   - **~28% Amazon** (the sole tech holding, and it's more of an infrastructure/AI play)
   - **~1% X-energy** (nuclear microreactors — a speculative energy bet)
   - **Zero exposure** to the MAG7 names he previously held (NVDA, MSFT, TSLA, ASML)

4. **Vistra Corp is the only carryover:** Vistra Corp (symbol: VST) was held in Q2 2025 (208,747 shares), Q3 2025 (208,747 shares), and reappears in Q2 2026 with 372,755 shares (nearly doubled). The Q4 2025 sale and Q2 2026 repurchase suggest a tactical trade rather than continuous conviction.

5. **New Vistra Energy Position:** Vista Energy S.A.B. de C.V. (NYSE: VIST) is a separate Mexican/Latin American energy company — distinct from Vistra Corp (VST). This is a new addition.
