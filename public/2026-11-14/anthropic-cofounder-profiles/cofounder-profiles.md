## Overview

Four researchers, a former journalist, a public policymaker, and an engineer founded Anthropic in 2021. Six years later, the company is about to go public in one of the largest IPOs on record.

### Origin Story

At the end of 2020, siblings **Dario and Daniela Amodei** — both VPs at OpenAI — grew frustrated with the company's approach to AI safety. Fearing AI capabilities would outpace understanding, they left and took five OpenAI colleagues with them:

- **Tom Brown** — research engineering lead on GPT-3
- **Jack Clark** — policy director
- **Jared Kaplan** — contract research consultant
- **Sam McCandlish** — technical staff
- **Chris Olah** — technical staff

> "They really started Anthropic not because they were hoping to get rich, but they started Anthropic because they wanted to shape this really important thing in the world, and they thought that other people might do it worse than them."
> — **Jeffrey Ladish**, former Anthropic contract safety engineer, now executive director of Palisade Research

**Note:** Benjamin Mann (Anthropic technical staff, ex-OpenAI) is often called a co-founder but is **not** an owner of Founder LLC and lacks the same voting/equity rights.

## The Seven Co-Founders

### 1. Dario Amodei — CEO

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Age** | 43 |
| **Role** | Chief Executive Officer |
| **Shares** | 245,259,100 (most of any co-founder, includes voting proxies) |
| **Hometown** | San Francisco, CA (Mission District) |
| **Education** | PhD, Computational Neuroscience — Princeton University (2011) |
| **Postdoc** | Stanford University (2011–2014) |
| **Career** | Baidu — speech recognition research (2014–2015) → Google Brain — deep learning & neural networks → OpenAI (July 2016) |
| **Key contribution** | Co-invented **RLHF** (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) with Tom Brown |
| **Motivation** | "I got into AI to solve the 'almost intractable' and 'slow moving' problems of biology." (Big Technology podcast, July 2025) |

**Notable quote:**
> "I think my reason to start a company was that I had worked at other companies and I just didn't feel like the way those companies were run was really oriented towards trying to have that impact."

**Background:** Dario is the face of Anthropic. His entire career has been research-focused — from computational neuroscience at Princeton to deep learning at Google Brain. He joined OpenAI in 2016, where he and Tom Brown developed RLHF, now a foundational alignment technique used across the industry. He has said that companies often use "impact" as an insincere recruiting ploy, and that he founded Anthropic to genuinely pursue it.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Age** | 39 |
| **Role** | Chief Compute Officer |
| **Shares** | ~240M Class A shares |
| **Education** | Master of Engineering — MIT (2010) |

Grouper was a YC-backed dating app. At OpenAI, he led the training of GPT-3 — a historic leap in model scale that helped kick off the current AI era. Now he oversees Anthropic's compute infrastructure.

| Attribute | Detail |

**Notable quote (The Guardian, Dec 2025):**

**Background:** Still holds his faculty position at Johns Hopkins. Kaplan co-developed the scaling laws that showed LLMs improve predictably with more data and compute — a finding that directly enabled the race to larger models. He also created Constitutional AI, Anthropic's method for aligning models using a written set of safety principles. His day-to-day focuses on safety assessments, responsible scaling policy, and existential risk.

He now runs Anthropic's interpretability research — attempting to peer inside the "black box" of large language models. He was the most resistant to forming a for-profit company.

## Governance & Philanthropy

- **Founder LLC** — owns a single Class F share granting **50.1% majority voting control** to the seven co-founders, despite their collective ~10% economic stake.
- Benjamin Mann, a member of Anthropic's technical staff and ex-OpenAI employee, is often called a co-founder but is **not an owner of Founder LLC**.
- Co-founders will collectively sell **over 20 million shares** during the IPO (~$250M cash each).
- **80% of personal Anthropic equity** pledged to philanthropy (stated in IPO filing; **not legally binding**).
- Anthropic is setting aside **5% of shares** for a public benefit fund — to finance nonprofits, research, and community grants — with potential to grow as the company grows.
