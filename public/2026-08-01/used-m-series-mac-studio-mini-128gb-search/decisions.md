# Decisions — Used M-Series Mac Studio / Mac mini 128GB+ Search

## 2026-08-01

### Decision: Use Swappa as the grounded marketplace source
- **Context:** eBay, B&H, and some generic search tooling were blocked or challenged during this session.
- **Options considered:** eBay search, Bing/DDG search, B&H, Swappa.
- **Decision:** Use Swappa listing/category pages because they were accessible and exposed live filters plus listing data.
- **Consequence:** Results are grounded in one live marketplace; broader coverage would require additional manual search on blocked sites.
