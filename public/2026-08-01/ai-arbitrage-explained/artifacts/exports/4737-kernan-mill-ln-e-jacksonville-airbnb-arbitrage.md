## Regulatory screen
- **Florida state law:** Fla. Stat. **509.032(7)(b)** says a local law, ordinance, or regulation **may not prohibit vacation rentals or regulate the duration or frequency of rental of vacation rentals**, except older pre-2011 local laws.
- **Duval County tourist tax:** Duval County imposes **6% Convention/Tourist Development Tax** on short-term rentals and states the owner remains responsible for collection/remittance.
- **Transient rental tax stack:** Duval says the **6% TDT + 7.5% state sales/use tax = 13.5% total transient-rental tax**.
- **Local business tax:** Jacksonville/Duval says if you own or operate a business in Duval County, you **must obtain a Local Business Tax Receipt (LBTR)**; it applies to home-based and one-person operations too.
