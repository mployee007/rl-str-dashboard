## Methodology & Assumptions

- **Rent baseline:** Cleveland-Elyria MSA FY2025 HUD Fair Market Rent, 3BR = $1,553/mo. Parma submarket adjustment: 90% → $1,398/mo for 3BR, $988/mo for 2BR.
- **Gross yield formula:** (monthly rent × 12) / purchase price. Ignores vacancy, maintenance, capex, management, taxes, and insurance — this is a first-pass screen, not a pro forma.
- **Tax gap formula:** (ask − county assessed) / ask × 100. Uses Zillow `taxAssessedValue` which appears to reflect Cuyahoga County's full market-value estimate (not the 35% taxable value).
- **Data freshness:** All listings pulled live via Zillow `__NEXT_DATA__` at ~2026-10-04 00:00 UTC. DOM values are relative to pull time.
