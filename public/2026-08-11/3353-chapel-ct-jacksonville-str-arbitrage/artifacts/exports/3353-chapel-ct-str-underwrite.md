## Executive verdict

**Verdict: PASS as currently offered.**

This house matches your **house-first** preference and Jacksonville is a **regulatorily workable** Florida market, but this lead does **not** clear the bar for a clean arbitrage play in its current form.

### Why it misses
1. **Landlord-permission risk is the biggest issue.** The home is professionally managed by **American Homes 4 Rent**, which is exactly the kind of institutional SFR operator that often has tighter lease controls than mom-and-pop landlords. I could not verify a public lease clause from the website, so this needs **written approval for STR / subleasing** before it is even actionable.
2. **The moderate case is too thin.** On a realistic underwriting pass, the gross spread works, but once you layer in typical fixed monthly STR overhead for an unfurnished house, the deal is basically flat-to-negative.
3. **It is unfurnished.** That adds startup friction and either a cash furnishing hit or an ongoing furnishing reserve.
4. **The location is more suburban than destination-led.** This is not a premium beach / downtown / resort node, so you cannot underwrite it like Jacksonville Beach or a waterfront trophy listing.
