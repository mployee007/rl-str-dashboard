## Qualitative observations
- Both companies benefit from secular tailwinds: AI compute, advanced node complexity, chiplet/multi-die packaging, automotive electronics, hyperscale/datacenter design, and simulation-heavy workflows.
- Cadence appears to have the cleaner near-term story: strong margins, solid backlog, and fewer mega-integration risks.
- Synopsys appears to have the broader strategic stack: leading EDA, strong design IP, and now much deeper simulation/analysis capabilities after Ansys.
- Synopsys' GAAP numbers remain noisy due to amortization, restructuring, and acquisition/divestiture items; non-GAAP is a more useful lens for multi-year valuation.
