# Cadence vs Synopsys Investment Analysis — Decisions

## Primary verdict
**Prefer Synopsys (SNPS) over Cadence (CDNS) for a fresh 2–5 year investment today.**

## Why SNPS wins
1. **Broader platform moat**
   - Synopsys is positioned across EDA, silicon IP, and now simulation/analysis after Ansys.
   - That expands wallet share and increases switching costs across the silicon-to-systems stack.

2. **AI complexity is a bigger monetization lever**
   - AI chips, chiplets, high-bandwidth memory integration, packaging, power integrity, and physical AI all favor more tools per design cycle.
   - Synopsys is well positioned to monetize that broader complexity.

3. **Valuation is more attractive on a forward non-GAAP basis**
   - Cadence looks cleaner, but also pricier relative to its forward earnings power.
   - Synopsys currently offers stronger modeled upside from the present price.

4. **Ansys creates a strategic edge if integration succeeds**
   - The acquisition is not just additive revenue; it materially expands the category the company can own.
   - If management executes, Synopsys can become the default engineering software layer from chip design through multiphysics validation.

## Why not make CDNS the top pick
- Cadence is still a high-quality compounder and arguably the cleaner operator today.
- However, the current valuation leaves less room for error.
- Its narrower scope versus Synopsys also limits the size of the strategic upside if system-level simulation becomes a larger part of engineering budgets.

## What would change the verdict
- If Synopsys stumbles on Ansys integration, margin recovery, or export restrictions intensify, the gap narrows materially.
- If Cadence compounds backlog into sustained 20%+ EPS growth while maintaining premium margins, CDNS could outperform despite the richer multiple.
