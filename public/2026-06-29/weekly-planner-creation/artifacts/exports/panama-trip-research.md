## 1. The Neighborhood You Were Thinking Of: **El Cangrejo**

**El Cangrejo** is a bohemian neighborhood in Panama City described as having a **laid-back vibe** — exactly what you mentioned. Key details:

- **Not downtown/business district** — it's a residential area with tree-lined streets, not skyscrapers
- Known for **cafés, international restaurants, bars, and boutiques**
- Walkable, safe, and more relaxed than the high-rise areas (Obarrio, Punta Pacifica)
- Centrally located between the historic district (Casco Viejo) and the business district
- Has its own metro station (Estación El Cangrejo / Vía Argentina)
- Top-rated hotel in the area: **Gran Evenia Panamá Hotel**

**Other neighborhoods considered** (ruled out as too business-like):
- **Obarrio** — commercial core, skyscrapers, lots of restaurants but feels like a business district
- **Punta Patilla/Punta Pacifica** — luxury towers, bay views, but high-rise/modern feel
- **Marbella** — upscale along Avenida Balboa, more formal
- **Casco Viejo** — historic old town, charming but can be touristy/lively
