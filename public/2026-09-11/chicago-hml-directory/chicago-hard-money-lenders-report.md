## SECTION 3: Transactional Funding (For $0-Down Auction Play)

Transactional funding = same-day double-close. You contract with seller → funder wires purchase money → you immediately resell to your end buyer → funder gets repaid same day. **This is your "no capital" auction strategy.**

| 8 | **The Wholesaler's Toolbox** | thewholesalerstoolbox.com | Transactional funding + wholesaler resources. |

**How transactional funding works for auction:**
1. You win the auction bid (need 10% cash/certified check — you still need that, or a JV partner who provides it)
2. Transactional funder wires balance of purchase price
3. You do a same-day double-close to your end buyer
4. Funder is repaid + fee (typically 1–2% of loan amount)
5. You keep the spread

**⚠️ Catch:** You still need the **10% auction deposit** in certified funds. Transactional funding covers the balance, not the deposit. For true $0-out-of-pocket, you need a JV partner or credit partner to front the deposit.
