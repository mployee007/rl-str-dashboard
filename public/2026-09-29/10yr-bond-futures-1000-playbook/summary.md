## Question
How to trade the 10-year bond futures market with $1,000 of capital.

## Core finding
The standard 10-Year T-Note futures contract (**ZN**, $100,000 face) is **unaffordable
overnight** at $1,000 — CME maintenance margin is $1,875 (initial ~$2,063). At $1,000 you
are effectively a **day trader**: day margins on micros run $25–$200, but overnight margins
are 5–20× larger.

## What the PDF contains
1. The day-margin vs overnight-margin rule.
2. Day-trade instrument ranking (cheapest → priciest) with day + overnight margins.
3. Overnight-holdable list for a $1,000 account (overnight margin < $500).
4. **Expressing a 10-year rate view** — instrument table + directional logic.
5. **Starter position plan** — Micro 10-Year Yield futures (10Y): 1 contract, $330 margin,
   5 bp stop / 10 bp target, worked example.
6. Risk rules (PDT exemption for futures, margin reversion, commission drag) + broker checklist.

## Verdict delivered
$1,000 is a tuition account, not an income account. Trade 1 micro contract, risk $50,
prove the process in a simulator for 2 weeks first.
