## What was accomplished — findings
- The closest official fix article is Meta’s “Troubleshoot microphone issues on AI glasses,” which specifically covers glasses not responding consistently to “Hey Meta”.
- Meta says to verify the “Hey Meta” and Wake sound toggles, run Microphone troubleshooting / Microphone adjustment, then unpair/re-pair, then factory reset, then contact Meta Store Support.
- Meta’s retailer page lists US authorized retailers for Ray-Ban Meta including Best Buy, AT&T, B&H, Microcenter, Target, T-Mobile, Verizon, and Walmart.
- Meta’s support portal explicitly supports “AI glasses software and app” and “Device and accessory issues” for Ray-Ban Meta / Oakley Meta / Meta Ray-Ban Display.
