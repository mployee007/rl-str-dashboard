# Meta Glasses: “Hey Meta” not working

## Best diagnosis
Most likely causes, in order:
1. **Wake word got toggled off** in the Meta AI app.
2. **Microphone issue / mic calibration issue**.
3. **App pairing or permissions issue** after an update.
4. **Firmware/app glitch** that needs re-pairing or factory reset.
5. **Hardware fault** if voice still fails after reset and mic troubleshooting.

## Official fixes to try first
From Meta’s official troubleshooting article:
1. Open the **Meta AI mobile app**.
2. Tap **Glasses** → **Device settings**.
3. Tap **Meta AI** → **“Hey Meta” preferences**.
4. Make sure **“Hey Meta”** is ON.
5. Make sure **Wake sound** is ON.
6. Test with a simple command like: **“Hey Meta, how much battery is left?”**
   - If the glasses hear you, the **notification LED should blink white**.
7. If still failing, turn **“Hey Meta”** and **Wake sound** OFF, then back ON.
8. Go to **Device settings** → **Microphone troubleshooting**.
9. Turn on **Microphone adjustment** and test again.
10. If still broken, **unpair and re-pair** the glasses.
11. If still broken after that, do a **factory reset**.
12. If it still fails after reset, **contact Meta Store Support**.

## Quick triage I’d use
| Check | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Music/calls sound normal? | Speakers/Bluetooth likely okay |
| People hear you poorly on calls too? | Strong sign of microphone problem |
| White LED blinks when you say “Hey Meta”? | Mics hear you; problem may be app/AI response, not hardware |
| Works with tap-and-hold but not wake phrase? | Wake-word setting/config issue more likely |
| Fails even after re-pair + reset? | Hardware or deeper firmware issue |

## My recommendation
### Take
1. Try the **app toggle + microphone adjustment** steps first.
2. If it still fails, **unpair/re-pair**.
3. If still dead, **factory reset**.
4. If still dead after reset, go straight to **Meta Store Support** and, if you want same-day human help, stop by **Best Buy (555 W Roosevelt)** or **Micro Center (2645 N Elston)** with the glasses, case, proof of purchase, and app screenshots.

### Negotiate / ask for
- Warranty replacement or exchange if the mic/wake word still fails after reset.
- Confirmation whether they can test microphone input or process a support claim.

### Pass
- Random third-party electronics repair shops unless Meta/Ray-Ban explicitly directs you there. I didn’t find an official list of independent repair depots for these glasses.
